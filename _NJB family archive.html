Nicholas J. Barton has in his possession a veritable treasure trove of family history documents, including many old photographs, which have been accumulated over at least 4 generations. My goal is to convert as much of this to electronic form as possible (and desirable) and place the contents on this website, but I have only just begun.

All family portraits on this website, if not credited elsewhere, are taken from this archive, but it usually isn't possible to determine their provenance beyond that.
