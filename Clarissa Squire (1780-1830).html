• DBHB's great x4 grandmother.
  • Daughter of Joseph Squire and Elizabeth Spurrell.
  • Married, on 22 November 1798, John Dougan (1765-1826).
  • Mother of Emily Dougan (1806-1877) and Mary Stephen Dougan (1804-1881).
  • Died 11 May 1830, of dropsy.


Photo of miniature portrait from Tina Woodhead via Laurel Rockliff.

Mentions


In Emily's Memoir, p7:
"My Grandmother Dougan was known in Devon as one of the 3 beautiful Miss Squires; her Christian name was Clarissa. (On 2 separate papers given me by my cousin Sir Claude Macdonald - great nephew of my Mother's - you will find full particulars of the Dougan family)."
