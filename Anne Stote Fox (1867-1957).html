• Born 16 March 1867, daughter of Rev. Henry E. Fox.
  • Known as 'Annie'.
  • Married (on 23 December 1896 in Norbiton Church near Kingston), Fred Barton (1868-1929).
  • Died June 1957, aged 90.

Named after Rev. Frederick Gell, Bishop of Madras (1820-1902) (*Barton 1910)
