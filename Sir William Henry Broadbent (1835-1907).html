• Born January 1835, eldest son of John Broadbent (1796-1880) and Esther Butterworth (1810-1879).
  • Married (in August 1863 in Holmfirth) Eliza Harpin.
  • Famed neurologist and physician to the royal family, and the target of attempted extortion by the Lambeth Poisoner. Broadbent's Wikipedia entry is here.
  • Died 1907, aged ~72.
