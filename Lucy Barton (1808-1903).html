• Daughter of Bernard Barton the Quaker poet (1784-1849) and Lucy Jesup (d.1808)
  • Her mother died after childbirth and she was raised by her father.
  • After her father's death, married his friend Edward Fitzgerald (1809-1883).
  • Her name (as 'Lucy Fitzgerald') appears on the inscription of Bernard Barton of Carlisle (1728-1773)'s gravestone, as one of the 1888 restorers thereof along with Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908).
  • Died childless.
