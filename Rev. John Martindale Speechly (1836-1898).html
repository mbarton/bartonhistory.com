• Born 13 November 1836, son of Thomas Kelfull Speechly (1804-1884) and Sarah Bellers (1805-1842).
  • Vicar of Hernehill, Faversham.
  • Married Mary Gray Grove (b.1836).
  • Bishop of Travancore and Cochin.
  • Died 20 January 1898, aged 71. Buried in Whittlesey, Cambridgeshire.

Genealogical information found here.

Bishop Speechly was a friend of the Bartons in India, and is mentioned in the *Memoirs of Emily Elliott.

Bishop Speechly was also (presumably by coincidence) related to Mary Bushby (4th cousin twice removed), who married Lt. Col. Ted Barton (1897-1971).

Even more curiously, my own wife is a Speechley, whose ancestry traces back to Whittlesey, as does the Bishop's.
