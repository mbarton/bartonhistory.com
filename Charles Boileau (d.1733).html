• DBHB's great x7 grandfather.
  • Born in either 1670 or 1673, son of Jacques Boileau (1626-1697) and Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700).
  • Descendant of William the Conqueror through his mother Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700)
  • Father of Simeon Boileau (1717-1767).
  • Died 7 March 1733, aged ~60.
