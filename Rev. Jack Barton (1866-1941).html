• Born 22 September 1866, second son of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) and Emily Elliott (1839-1924).
  • Full name 'John Bernard Barton' but usually known as 'Jack' (or 'Johnnie' in his mother's memoirs).
  • Married, on 31 July 1895 at St. Nicholas Church, Nottingham, Susan Thornton (1875-1958).
  • Died 12 September 1941, aged 74.
