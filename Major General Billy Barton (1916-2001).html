• First cousin of Ronald Barton (1901-1986)
  • Born January 17 1916, youngest son of Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941) and Susan Thornton (1875-1958).
  • Christened 'Francis Christopher Barton', but nick-named Billy Barton after a race horse.
  • Educated at Haileybury (the last Barton to go there), Batten House.
  • Commissioned into the Royal Marines in 1934.
  • Married (on October 4 1939 in Plymouth) Olivia Mary Darroll-Smith (Molly).
  • Kept a fascinating diary throughout World War 2, during which he travelled the oceans mainly on the HMS Enterprise and the HMS Nigeria.
  • Served in 40 Commando in Malaya.
  • Commanded 45 Commando in Cyprus (1958-1960).
  • Died 2001, aged ~85.
