• DBHB's grandmother.
  • Born 2 August 1907, only child of George Farrell (1872-1956) and Sarah Carty (1874-1937).
  • Always known as 'Molly'.
  • Her father left his family when Molly was young. He went abroad.
  • Married (on 7 June 1930 in Camberwell, London) Ronald Barton (1901-1986).

  • Died 8 September 1999, aged 92.
