• Born 16 May 1843, daughter of John Samuel Lister.
  • Married (on 21 May 1862), Charles Pearson Elliott (1833-1876).
  • Known in Emily's memoir as 'Aunt Fannie'.
  • Married secondly (on 5 June 1878) Rev. Francis Littler.

A Mr. Littler is mentioned in Emily's memoir pp66-67.

Children

  2. Charles Lister Boileau Elliott (1864-1940).
  3. Alicia Mary Elliott (b.&d.1866).
  4. Arthur Edward Elliott (b.1867).
  5. Edith Mary Elliott (b.1869).
