• Born 12 August 1799, son of Anne Boileau (1769-1854) and John Reid.
  • Governor of Bombay. Left India in 1844. Awarded M.C.S..
  • Married firstly Emily Rogers, secondly his second cousin Jane Boileau.
  • Died 27 October 1878, aged 79.

Jane Boileau was daughter of Samuel Brandrum Boileau (1801-1860), who was son of John Theophilus Boileau (1755-1817), brother of Solomon Boileau (1745-1810)
