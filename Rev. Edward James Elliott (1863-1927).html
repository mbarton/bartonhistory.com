• Born 6 October 1863, youngest son of Rev. Edward King Elliott (1828-1920) and Mary Elizabeth Richey.
  • Married in 1894 Edith Helen Willey.
  • Died 15 February 1927, aged 63. Buried at Broadwater and Worthing Cemetery.

Gravestone


This image of his shared gravestone was kindly provided by Mrs Christina Taylor of the The Friends of Broadwater & Worthing Cemetery (www.fbwc.co.uk/):



The text reads:

"In ever precious memory of Edward James Elliott M.A.
Rector of Broadwater (1905-1927) who entered into life February 15th 1927.

And now Lord what is my Hope:? Truly my Hope is even in Thee.

--

Upon a Life. I did not Live.
Upon a Death. I did not Die.
Another's Life, another's Death
I stake my whole Eternity.

Also Edith Helen
Beloved wife of the above who joined him Nov. 5th 1942
The darling father amd mother of Charteris Elliott

"Until"_ then for all Eternity

And their son
Charteris Frederick Venn Richey Elliott
Died and buried in Tangier January 5th 1969."
