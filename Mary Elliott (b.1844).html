• Born 29 September 1844, youngest child of Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875) and Emily Dougan (1806-1877).
  • Full name 'Harriet Mary Elliott' but always known as 'Mary' (or 'Aunt Mary').
  • Married, on 16 August 1881, Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920), a good friend of her brother-in-law Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908).




Children


With Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920):
  1. Tesie Moule (1882-1905).
  2. Isabel Catherine Moule (1884-1959).
