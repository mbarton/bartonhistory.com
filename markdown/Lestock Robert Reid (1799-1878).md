---
title: "Lestock Robert Reid (1799-1878)"
---

- Born 12 August 1799, son of [Anne Boileau (1769-1854)](Anne%20Boileau%20%281769-1854%29.md) and [John Reid](John%20Reid.md).
- Governor of Bombay. Left India in 1844. Awarded M.C.S..
- Married firstly Emily Rogers, secondly his second cousin [Jane Boileau](Jane%20Boileau.md).
- Died 27 October 1878, aged 79.

Jane Boileau was daughter of [Samuel Brandrum Boileau (1801-1860)](Samuel%20Brandrum%20Boileau%20%281801-1860%29.md), who was son of [John Theophilus Boileau (1755-1817)](John%20Theophilus%20Boileau%20%281755-1817%29.md), brother of [Solomon Boileau (1745-1810)](Solomon%20Boileau%20%281745-1810%29.md)
