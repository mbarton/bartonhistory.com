---
title: "Fred Barton (1868-1929)"
---

- Born 28 September 1868, son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Full name 'Percy Frederick Barton', but always known as 'Fred'.
- Married (on 23 December 1896 in Norbiton Church near Kingston), [Anne Stote Fox (1867-1957)](Anne%20Stote%20Fox%20%281867-1957%29.md).
- Died May 1929, aged 60.

Named after Rev. Frederick Gell, Bishop of Madras (1820-1902) ([\*Barton 1910](_Barton%201910.md))
