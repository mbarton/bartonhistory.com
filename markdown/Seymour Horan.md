- Married [Molly Causton (b.1871)](Molly%20Causton%20%28b.1871%29.md).

"*In August we went over the [Honister Pass](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honister_Pass), to [Buttermere](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buttermere,_Cumbria) Vicarage, into which nutshell of a house six of us packed; while [Guy](Guy%20Barton%20%281875-1947%29.md) and [Will](Bill%20Barton%20%281877-1952%29.md), with [Seymour Horan](Seymour%20Horan.md), had rooms out. Father was responsible for the duty there this month, in the very tiny Church; and it was a moving sight for me to see him assisted in the service by his two sons in Holy Orders. Aunt [Emily](Emily%20Barton%20%281842-1927%29.md) and [Alice](Alice%20Matilda%20Barton%20%28b.1862%29.md) put up at the hotel at Buttermere for a week, and thus enlarged our family party; and we had Tea picnics at [Crummock Water](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crummock_Water), and elsewhere; and all-day walking excursions. Ever since this summer Seymour has been more or less a member of the family, and quasi-brother to you all and my 'seventh son'; and as I write, we are within a few days of his entering the family as a Nephew.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p102](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#102).
