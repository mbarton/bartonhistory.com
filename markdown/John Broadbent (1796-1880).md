---
title: "John Broadbent (1796-1880)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x3 grandfather.
- Born 11 March 1796, son of [John Broadbent (1758-1837)](John%20Broadbent%20%281758-1837%29.md) and [Mary Dyson (d.1805)](Mary%20Dyson%20%28d.1805%29.md).
- Contemporary of [Emperor Wilhelm I of Germany (1797-1888)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_I_of_Germany) and [Mary Shelley (1797-1851)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Shelley).
- Married (on 17 May 1832, in Longwood, Huddersfield), [Esther Butterworth (1810-1879)](Esther%20Butterworth%20%281810-1879%29.md).
- Died 1880, aged ~84.
