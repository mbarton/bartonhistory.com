---
title: "Eling Mary Elliott (b.1833)"
---

- Born 21 April 1833, daughter of [Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875)](Rev.%20Edward%20Bishop%20Elliott%20%281793-1875%29.md) and Mary King.
- Named after her grandmother [Eling Venn (1758-1843)](Eling%20Venn%20%281758-1843%29.md).
- Married, on 1 July 1856, [Rev. Clement Frances Cobb (1827-1896)](Rev.%20Clement%20Frances%20Cobb%20%281827-1896%29.md)

She was a first (half-)cousin of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and thus the second (half-)cousin once removed of his daughter [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md). Her son Frank married Emily Gertrude Causton (b.1869), who was [Emily](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md)'s [husband](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md)'s niece.

# Children
