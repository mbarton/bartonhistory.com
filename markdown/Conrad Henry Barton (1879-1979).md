---
title: "Conrad Henry Barton (1879-1979)"
---

- Born 1 September 1879 in Freiburg, Germany, youngest son of [Rev. Gerard Barton (1834-1889)](Rev.%20Gerard%20Barton%20%281834-1889%29.md) and Elizabeth Hazard.
- Full name "Conrad Henry Barton"
- Died 8 June 1979, aged 99.
