---
title: "Lucy Barton (1808-1903)"
---

- Daughter of [Bernard Barton the Quaker poet (1784-1849)](Bernard%20Barton%20the%20Quaker%20poet%20%281784-1849%29.md) and Lucy Jesup (d.1808)
- Her mother died after childbirth and she was raised by her father.
- After her father's death, married his friend [Edward Fitzgerald (1809-1883)](Famous%20connections.md).
- Her name (as 'Lucy Fitzgerald') appears on the inscription of [Bernard Barton of Carlisle (1728-1773)](Bernard%20Barton%20of%20Carlisle%20%281728-1773%29.md)'s [gravestone](Bernard%20Barton%20of%20Carlisle%20%281728-1773%29.md#gravestone), as one of the 1888 restorers thereof along with [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
- Died childless.
