---
title: "Frances Wright (1840-1914)"
---

- Born 1840, daughter of [Francis Wright (1806-1873)](Francis%20Wright%20%281806-1873%29.md) and [Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888)](Selina%20FitzHerbert%20%281806-1888%29.md).
- Known to [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) as 'Fanny', and is mentioned in his [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md).
- Married (on 31 May 1859, at Osmaston near Ashbourne, Derbyshire), [Rev. Fred Wigram (1834-1897)](Rev.%20Fred%20Wigram%20%281834-1897%29.md). It was a double wedding with her sister [Elizabeth Wright (d.1909)](Elizabeth%20Wright%20%28d.1909%29.md), and [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) attended and described it in his [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md).
- Died 16 February 1914, aged 74.
