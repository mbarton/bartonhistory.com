---
title: "Mary Elliott (b.1844)"
---

- Born 29 September 1844, youngest child of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Full name 'Harriet Mary Elliott' but always known as 'Mary' (or 'Aunt Mary').
- Married, on 16 August 1881, [Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920)](Rev.%20Handley%20Moule%20%281841-1920%29.md), a good friend of her brother-in-law [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
