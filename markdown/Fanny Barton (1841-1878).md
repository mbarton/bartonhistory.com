---
title: "Fanny Barton (1841-1878)"
---

- Born 25 September 1841[[1]](#cite_note-1) , daughter of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Married (on 10 July 1866 at Croydon in Surrey)[[2]](#cite_note-2) , [Rev. Claude Cecil Thornton (1844-1921)](Rev.%20Claude%20Cecil%20Thornton%20%281844-1921%29.md).
- Died in 1878, aged 37.

![Fanny Barton b 1841 m Rev C C Thornton.jpg](../files/Fanny%20Barton%20b%201841%20m%20Rev%20C%20C%20Thornton.jpg "Fanny Barton b 1841 m Rev C C Thornton.jpg")

"*After the funeral we left Buxton, and spent the remainder of July at Matlock; Aunt Fannie was with us, and two of her children. August at Retford, in Nottinghamshire; and September at [Southwold](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=southwold&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Southwold,+Suffolk,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=7UPyTLqbEsHChAe91-XxCw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CCIQ8gEwAA&z=13), where [Uncle Claude Thornton](Rev.%20Claude%20Cecil%20Thornton%20%281844-1921%29.md), and your **Aunt Fanny** his wife, then lived at the Vicarage. She was a very fine woman, both in looks and in character; and her five children were of the same ages as our, from Jack to Jessie. I think this was the last time we saw her - for her children alas! were left motherless less than 18 months later.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p81](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#81)

Her husband [Rev. Claude Cecil Thornton (1844-1921)](Rev.%20Claude%20Cecil%20Thornton%20%281844-1921%29.md) was the brother of [Henry Edward Thornton (1842-1926)](Henry%20Edward%20Thornton%20%281842-1926%29.md), whose daughter [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md) married [Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941)](Rev.%20Jack%20Barton%20%281866-1941%29.md). In other words Fanny's nephew (Jack) married Fanny's husband's niece (Susan).

After Fanny's death, Claude remarried (on 19 September 1882 in High Cross, Ware, Hertfordshire) [Alice Henrietta Giles Puller (1852-1930)](Alice%20Henrietta%20Giles%20Puller%20%281852-1930%29.md), with whom he had eight more children.
