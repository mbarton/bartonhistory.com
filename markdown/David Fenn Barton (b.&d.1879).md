---
title: "David Fenn Barton (b.&d.1879)"
---

- Born 30 August 1879, youngest son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Named after family friend 'David Fenn'.
- Died the same day.
