---
title: "Charles Boileau (d.1733)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x7 grandfather.
- Born in either 1670 or 1673, son of [Jacques Boileau (1626-1697)](Jacques%20Boileau%20%281626-1697%29.md) and [Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700)](Fran%C3%A7oise%20de%20Vignolles%20%281643-1700%29.md).
- [Descendant of William the Conqueror through his mother](Royal%20ancestry.md#william-the-conqueror-1028-1087-and) [Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700)](Fran%C3%A7oise%20de%20Vignolles%20%281643-1700%29.md)
- Father of [Simeon Boileau (1717-1767)](Simeon%20Boileau%20%281717-1767%29.md).
- Died 7 March 1733, aged ~60.
