[Srinagar](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srinagar) is the capital of the most northern Indian state [Jammu and Kashmir](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jammu_and_Kashmir), and lies only 100 miles ENE of the Pakistani capital [Islamabad](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamabad). The city lies on the [Jhelum River](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jhelum_River) next to the [Dal Lake](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dal_Lake)and is famous for its many waterways and houseboats. A large hill once called Takt-e-suleiman (now called Shankaracharya Hill) looms over the city, and on one summit sits the [Shankaracharya temple](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shankaracharya_temple).

[Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) lived in Srinagar for several years with his young family, and was the vicar of All Saints Church, a church which still exists albeit in a more modern building.

# Barton house

![Cecil Bartons house in Srinagar Jan 1901 Cecil and Esther in ponytrap.jpg](../files/Cecil%20Bartons%20house%20in%20Srinagar%20Jan%201901%20Cecil%20and%20Esther%20in%20ponytrap.jpg "Cecil Bartons house in Srinagar Jan 1901 Cecil and Esther in ponytrap.jpg")\
The Barton house in Srinagar (near All Saints Church). [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and [Esther](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md) sit in the ponytrap in front of the house.

The precise location of this house isn't yet known, but a commenter called Jehangir on [this Kashmiri blog](http://autarmota.blogspot.co.uk/2010/07/material-received-from-davis-barton.html) notes that "*In the year 1962 the J&K State Government leased out Revd. Biscoe's and Revd. Barton's residences situated at Sheikh Bagh, Srinagar, to the [C.M.S Tyndale Biscoe Memorial High School](http://biscoe.kauls.net/) for a period of 40 years.*" The location of this school is shown below:
