---
title: "Ethel Emma Louise Reid (1860-1900)"
---

- Born 29 September 1860, daughter of [Lestock Robert Reid (1799-1878)](Lestock%20Robert%20Reid%20%281799-1878%29.md) and [Jane Boileau](Jane%20Boileau.md).
- Married a vicar in England.
- Died 10 June 1900, aged 39.

"*It was a sad break in our party when [Lilford](Lilford%20Jervoise%20Causton%20%28b.1873%29.md) was sent to return to England on account of the death of his Vicar's wife, a cousin of mine, née **Ethel Reid**.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p120](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#120)
