---
title: "Emily Frances Barton (b.&d.1860)"
---

- Born 17 March 1860, only daughter of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and his first wife [Catherine Wigram (1831-1860)](Catherine%20Wigram%20%281831-1860%29.md).
- Died 13 June 1860, aged nearly 3 months.

"*Shortly after I went for a little quiet rest to my favourite haunt at [Rydal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rydal,_Cumbria), [Knab Cottage](http://www.amblesideonline.co.uk/display/nabcottage/main.html), accompanied by my sister [Fanny](Fanny%20Barton%20%281841-1878%29.md). The sweet peacefulness of that lovely valley was very soothing to my spirit and helped me to bear the further sorrow which it pleased God to send upon me. I think of June 13th* [1860] *in the removal of **my little lamb** to join her [mother](Catherine%20Wigram%20%281831-1860%29.md). She had always been a fragile little thing and had doubtless inherited her mother’s chest delicacy so we could not but feel that it had been ordered graciously and well – though it was sharp pang at the moment.*" [\*Memoirs of John Barton](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md)
