---
title: "Rev. Spencer Ruthven Thornton (1813-1850)"
---

- Born 12 October 1813, son of [Claude George Thornton (1776-1866)](Claude%20George%20Thornton%20%281776-1866%29.md) and [Frances Anne Smith (1785-1862)](Frances%20Anne%20Smith%20%281785-1862%29.md).
- Married (on 7 September 1839 at Holy Trinity Church, Marylebone) [Caroline Adelaide Dupre (1815-1898)](Caroline%20Adelaide%20Dupre%20%281815-1898%29.md).
- Died 12 January 1850, aged 36.

"*In May* [1894] *I had found myself seated at the C.M.S. Annual Meeting, at Exeter Hall, behind Mr. H. E. Thornton, and spoke to him for the first time though we had known and loved his mother, Mrs. **Spencer Thornton**, & sisters at Broxbourne for years.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p106](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#106)
