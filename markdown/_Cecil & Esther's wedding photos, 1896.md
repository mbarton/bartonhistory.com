Cecil & Esther's wedding on 21 October 1896 at [Murree, now in Pakistan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murree). Click on the image to download a larger version.

The caption at the top reads "*BARTON:BROADBENT.--On the 21st Oct., at Murree, Punjaub, By the Right Reverend the [Bishop of Lahore](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_James_Matthew), the [REV. CECIL E. BARTON](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md), Church Missionary Society, Mooltan, son of the [Rev. John Barton](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md), Secy. Church Pastoral Aid Society, to [ESTHER MARY](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md), only daughter of [LT.-COLONEL BROADBENT](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md), R.E. (By telegram.)*".

[Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) & [Esther](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md) are easily identifiable at the centre: he in a dog collar, she in a veil.

Cecil's brother [Arthur Elliott Barton (1864-1931)](Arthur%20Elliott%20Barton%20%281864-1931%29.md) is the gentleman with a receding hairline and moustache standing in the back row just to the right of the highest/tallest soldier.

Esther's father [Col. John Edward Broadbent (1845-1931)](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) is the bald gentleman seated to the right of the bridesmaid who stands to the right of Esther. Her mother [Dora Nicholson (1844-1897)](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) (who died only eight months after the wedding) is the lady seated (with a bouquet) to the left of the bridesmaid standing to the left of Cecil.

# Guests

Mr. Martin Smith of Surrey contacted me and has helped to identify some of the guests. The distinguished and decorated gentleman standing a few people to the left of the bride surrounded by a large number of women is believed to be [General Sir William Stephen Alexander Lockhart (1841-1900)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lockhart_(Indian_Army_officer)), who became Commander-in-Chief of India two years after this photo was taken. According to Mr. Smith, the single medal on his right breast in the wedding photo is from the [Royal Humane Society](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Humane_Society).

Mr Smith's book about General Lockhart can be ordered at <http://www.amazon.co.uk/General-William-Stephen-Alexander-Lockhart/dp/0957015402>.

Esther's father [Col. John Edward Broadbent (1845-1931)](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) served under Lockhart, as Chief Engineer with the rank of Brigadier-General, on the [Tirah expedition](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirah_Campaign) of 1897-1898, not long after this wedding. It was for this that he was awarded his [C.B.](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Bath).

"**Bishop Matthews**" was the man who married them, according to Emily's memoir, [p112](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#112):

*"On October 21* [1896] *Cecil and Esther were married at [Murree](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murree), in India, by good [Bishop Matthews of Lahore](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_James_Matthew). [Arthur](Arthur%20Elliott%20Barton%20%281864-1931%29.md) was alas! the only one to represent the family, and he sent us a delightful account of that happy and festive occasion. [Colonel](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) & [Mrs. Broadbent](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) grudged nothing to their sweet and only daughter - and the Mother (our friend [Dora Nicholson](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) of early Calcutta days) quite accepted Cecil as a son, during the eight months she lived after this marriage - after which a sudden illness called her 'Home'\*. After a brief honeymoon this young couple settled down in Multan to work there for two years.*

*(\*In June, 1897. Two months later Colonel Broadbent having gone to the Malakand Campaign as Chief Engineer, Esther travelled to Barnes Court, Simla, residence of 'Uncle [Willy' Mackworth Young](Sir%20William%20Mackworth%20Young%20%28b.1840%29.md), then Lieutenant-Governor of the Punjab, where [Ted](Lt.%20Col.%20Ted%20Barton%20%281897-1971%29.md) was born, on September 6th.)"*

"**Mr Warlow**" is presumably Edmund John Warlow, who became archdeacon of Lahore, India. His daughter **Primrose Warlow** ended up marrying [Valentine Vivian of MI6](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentine_Vivian).

Richard thinks that "**Mary**" is Mary Williams, sister of his grandmother. According to Richard, one of Mary's sons married [M. M. Kaye](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._M._Kaye), author of "[The Far Pavilions](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Far_Pavilions)".
