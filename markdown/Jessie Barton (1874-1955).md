---
title: "Jessie Barton (1874-1955)"
---

- Born 21 April 1874, daughter of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Full name 'Jessie Isabel Barton'.
- Never married.
- Died 5 February 1955, aged 80.
