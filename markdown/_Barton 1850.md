<u>**Memoir, Letters and Poems of [Bernard Barton](Bernard%20Barton%20the%20Quaker%20poet%20%281784-1849%29.md) (1850)** ed. Lucy Barton</u> (online [here](http://www.archive.org/details/memoirletterspoe00bartrich)).

Contains (pp13-42) the '[Memoir of Bernard Barton](_Fitzgerald%201849.md)' written by his friend [Edward Fitzgerald](Famous%20connections.md#Edward Fitzgerald), who soon after he married Bernard's daughter Lucy, the editor of this work. Also contains a number of letters written by Bernard (pp43-146) and some letters written to him (pp149-190), which are reproduced in a separate article [here](_Bernard%20Barton%20the%20Quaker%20Poet%20Correspondence.md).
