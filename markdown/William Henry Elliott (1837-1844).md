---
title: "William Henry Elliott (1837-1844)"
---

- Born 14 December 1837, fourth child of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Clearly named after his uncle [William Henry Elliott (1811-1870)](William%20Henry%20Elliott%20%281811-1870%29.md).
- Died 22 August 1844, aged 6. ([\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p13](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#13))
