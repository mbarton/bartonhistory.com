---
title: "Francis Beresford Wright (d.1911)"
---

- Son of [Francis Wright (1806-1873)](Francis%20Wright%20%281806-1873%29.md) and [Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888)](Selina%20FitzHerbert%20%281806-1888%29.md).
- Good friend of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and mentioned repeatedly in his [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md) as 'Frank'.
- Married, on 20 June 1862, his first cousin Adeline Frances Henrietta FitzHerbert.
- Died 1911.

I'm indebted to Charles Wright, a modern relative, for the details of his marriage and wife, and of a guest list - written down by Frank - of the [double wedding](Osmaston%20Manor%2C%20Derbyshire.md#double-wedding-1859) of his sisters Bessie and Fanny at Osmaston Manor in 1859.
