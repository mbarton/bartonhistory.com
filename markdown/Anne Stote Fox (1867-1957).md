---
title: "Anne Stote Fox (1867-1957)"
---

- Born 16 March 1867, daughter of [Rev. Henry E. Fox](Rev.%20Henry%20E.%20Fox.md).
- Known as 'Annie'.
- Married (on 23 December 1896 in Norbiton Church near Kingston), [Fred Barton (1868-1929)](Fred%20Barton%20%281868-1929%29.md).
- Died June 1957, aged 90.

Named after Rev. Frederick Gell, Bishop of Madras (1820-1902) ([\*Barton 1910](_Barton%201910.md))
