---
title: "John Barton of Newcastle-under-Lyme (1737-1836)"
---

- Born 1737, in Kirkmichael, Dumfries, Scotland.
- Married, on 7 February 1782, at St Mary and All Saints Church, Chesterfield, [Ann Malkin (b.1749)](Ann%20Malkin%20%28b.1749%29.md).
- Left Dumfries and moved to Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffordshire in 1757.
- Father of [John Barton of Dublin (1782-1874)](John%20Barton%20of%20Dublin%20%281782-1874%29.md).
- Died 1836, aged 99 years and 3 months.
- We currently know of no links to the Cumbrian Bartons.

Information on this John Barton and his descendants was provided by his descendant Stevan (contact [here](http://www.wikispaces.com/user/view/StevanA1)):

"*According to church records he moved ‘thence to **Ravensdale, Newry**’, Ireland. The marriage register states that John was from Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffs.*

*John and Ann were married at [St Mary and All Saints Church, Chesterfield](http://www.chesterfieldparishchurch.org.uk/)* [the church with the famous twisted spire] *by Rev. G. Bossley. The witnesses were Sarah Cooke and Sarah Malkin.*

*Ann's father was Rev. James Malkin, her brother was Robert Malkin, a wholesale linen merchant in Dublin. There is no record of the baptism of their children at Chesterfield. According to a letter written by a descendant, Catherine Moeran, John was a cloth merchant who settled in Newcastle, Staffordshire, where he married Ann. He died at age 99 yrs 3 mo. They had four known children and probably more.*"
