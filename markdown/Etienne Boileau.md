---
title: "Etienne Boileau"
---

- Claimed as an ancestor of [Jean Boileau (d.1396)](Jean%20Boileau%20%28d.1396%29.md) - supposedly his great great grandfather - making him [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x20 grandfather.
- Born either in 1200 or 1210.
- The earliest recorded 'Boileau', which may have led later Boileaus to invent a connection to him, though no supporting evidence now exists (according to the [\*Chronicles of Boileau](_Chronicles%20of%20Boileau.md)).
- [Provost](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provost_(civil)#France) of Paris from 1261, appointed by [King Louis IX of France (Saint Louis)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Louis_IX). Etienne's wikipedia entry is [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89tienne_Boileau).
- Died ~1269-1271.
