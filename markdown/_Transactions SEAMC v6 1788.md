**<u>Transactions of the Society Instituted at London for the Encouragement of Arts, Manufactures and Commerce, Volume 6 (1788)</u>** is online as part of [this volume](http://www.archive.org/details/transactionsofso05royauoft).

The 'Society for the Encouragement of Arts, Manufactures and Commerce', formed in 1754, became the [Royal Society of Arts](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_for_the_encouragement_of_Arts,_Manufactures_%26_Commerce) in 1847 (not to be confused with the slightly more prestigious [Royal Society of London for the Improvement of Natural Knowledge](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_society)).
