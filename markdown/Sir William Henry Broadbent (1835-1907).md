---
title: "Sir William Henry Broadbent (1835-1907)"
---

- Born January 1835, eldest son of [John Broadbent (1796-1880)](John%20Broadbent%20%281796-1880%29.md) and [Esther Butterworth (1810-1879)](Esther%20Butterworth%20%281810-1879%29.md).
- Married (in August 1863 in Holmfirth) [Eliza Harpin](Eliza%20Harpin.md).
- Famed neurologist and physician to the royal family, and the target of attempted extortion by the [Lambeth Poisoner](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Neill_Cream). Broadbent's Wikipedia entry is [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Broadbent).
- Died 1907, aged ~72.
