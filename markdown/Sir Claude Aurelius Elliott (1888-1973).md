---
title: "Sir Claude Aurelius Elliott (1888-1973)"
---

- Born 27 July 1888, son of [Sir Charles Alfred Elliott (1835-1911)](Sir%20Charles%20Alfred%20Elliott%20%281835-1911%29.md) and [Alice Louisa Gaussen (d.1930)](Alice%20Louisa%20Gaussen%20%28d.1930%29.md).
- Married, in 1913, [Gillian Dorothea Bloxam](Gillian%20Dorothea%20Bloxam.md).
- Headmaster of [Eton](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eton_College) during the Second World War (from 1933-1949); his Wikipedia entry is [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Aurelius_Elliott).
- Died 1973, aged ~85.

# Children

With [Gillian Dorothea Bloxam](Gillian%20Dorothea%20Bloxam.md):

1. Peter Richard Elliott (b.1914)
2. John Nicholas Rede Elliott (b.1916)
