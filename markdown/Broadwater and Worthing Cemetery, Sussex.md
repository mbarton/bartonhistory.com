All the information below was kindly provided by Mrs Christina Taylor of the The Friends of Broadwater & Worthing Cemetery ([www.fbwc.co.uk/](http://www.fbwc.co.uk/)).

Broadwater & Worthing Cemetery is the final resting place of [Rev. Edward King Elliott (1828-1920)](Rev.%20Edward%20King%20Elliott%20%281828-1920%29.md) and his wife [Mary Elizabeth Richey](Mary%20Elizabeth%20Richey.md), their daughters [Julia Louisa Richey Elliott (1855-1905)](Julia%20Louisa%20Richey%20Elliott%20%281855-1905%29.md) and [Alice Joan Emily Elliott (1857-1950)](Alice%20Joan%20Emily%20Elliott%20%281857-1950%29.md), and son [Rev. Edward James Elliott (1863-1927)](Rev.%20Edward%20James%20Elliott%20%281863-1927%29.md), and the latter's wife [Edith Helen Willey](Edith%20Helen%20Willey.md) and son [Charteris Frederick Venn Richey Elliott (1901-1969)](Charteris%20Frederick%20Venn%20Richey%20Elliott%20%281901-1969%29.md).

Although the headstone has been destroyed, records show that Alice is buried with one [Maria Emma Smith (1874-1956)](Maria%20Emma%20Smith%20%281874-1956%29.md), whose identity and relationship to Alice is a mystery to us. Please contact us if you know.

The two Reverend Edwards were rectors of the local church St. Mary's, the latter from 1905-1927, and the former for 52 years before that. Before that the rector for 56 years was Peter Wood, an uncle of Edward King Elliott. Wood's exact relationship is currently unknown but he might perhaps be connected in some way to Basil Woodd after whom Edward King Elliott's paternal uncle [Basil Wood Elliott (1799-1814)](Basil%20Wood%20Elliott%20%281799-1814%29.md) was named.

The locations of the two remaining headstones are marked on the following map:

![Edward King Elliott gravestone SMALL.JPG](../files/Edward%20King%20Elliott%20gravestone%20SMALL.JPG "Edward King Elliott gravestone SMALL.JPG")

The text reads:

"*Sacred to the dearly loved memory of **Julia Louisa Richey Elliott**. Second daughter of the Rev. E. K. & Mrs. Elliott who entered into the joy of her Lord at Broadwater Rectory February 27th 1905.*

*Sacred also to the universally beloved memory of **Mary**. Wife of the Rev. E. K. Elliott who departed this life at the Rectory to be with Christ: Novr. 2nd 1909.*

*"Her children arise up and call her blessed"*

*Also to the memory of the **Rev. E. K. Elliott**, for 52 years Rector of Broadwater, who passed away on Oct. 31st 1920. Aged 92 years.*"

According to Christina, the flint wall behind the above grave may have once delineated the Rectory's garden, or other land belonging to Edward King Elliott, who is thought to sold land to the cemetery board. Further information would be appreciated.

![Edward James Elliott gravestone SMALL.JPG](../files/Edward%20James%20Elliott%20gravestone%20SMALL.JPG "Edward James Elliott gravestone SMALL.JPG")

The text reads:

"*In ever precious memory of **Edward James Elliott** M.A.*\
*Rector of Broadwater (1905-1927) who entered into life February 15th 1927.*

*And now Lord what is my Hope:? Truly my Hope is even in Thee.*\
*--*\
*Upon a Life. I did not Live.*\
*Upon a Death. I did not Die.*\
*Another's Life, another's Death*\
*I stake my whole Eternity.*

*Also **Edith Helen***\
*Beloved wife of the above who joined him Nov. 5th 1942*\
*The darling father amd mother of Charteris Elliott*

*"Until"_ then for all Eternity*

*And their son*\
***Charteris Frederick Venn Richey Elliott***\
*Died and buried in Tangier January 5th 1969.*"
