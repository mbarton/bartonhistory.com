---
title: "Elizabeth Wright (d.1909)"
---

- Daughter of [Francis Wright (1806-1873)](Francis%20Wright%20%281806-1873%29.md) and [Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888)](Selina%20FitzHerbert%20%281806-1888%29.md).
- Married (on 31 May 1859 at Osmaston Manor, Derbyshire), [John Bridges Plumptre (1832-1888)](John%20Bridges%20Plumptre%20%281832-1888%29.md). It was a double wedding with her sister [Frances Wright (1840-1914)](Frances%20Wright%20%281840-1914%29.md), and [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) attended and described it in his [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md).
- Died 2 April 1909.
