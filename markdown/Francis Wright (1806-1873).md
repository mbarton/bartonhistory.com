---
title: "Francis Wright (1806-1873)"
---

- Born 21 December 1806, son of [John Wright (1758-1840)](John%20Wright%20%281758-1840%29.md) and [Elizabeth Beresford (1762-1833)](Elizabeth%20Beresford%20%281762-1833%29.md).
- Married (on 12 August 1830) [Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888)](Selina%20FitzHerbert%20%281806-1888%29.md).
- Lived at [Osmaston Manor, Derbyshire](Osmaston%20Manor%2C%20Derbyshire.md).
- Died 21 February 1873, aged 66.

![external image 304779_001.jpg](http://www.thepeerage.com/304779_001.jpg "external image 304779_001.jpg")\
Mr Francis Wright of [Osmaston Manor](Osmaston%20Manor%2C%20Derbyshire.md). He was [High Sheriff of Nottinghamshire](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Sheriff_of_Nottinghamshire) in 1842.
