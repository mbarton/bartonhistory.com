---
title: "Thomas Barton (1833-1893)"
---

- Born ~1833, son of [William Barton (1785-1846)](William%20Barton%20%281785-1846%29.md) and [Mary Irving (1804-1890)](Mary%20Irving%20%281804-1890%29.md).
- Possibly known as "Bowman Barton" (see [ThePeerage.com where he is so named](http://thepeerage.com/p31539.htm#i315383)).
- [Supposedly](http://thepeerage.com/p31539.htm#i315383) lived at Botcherby Grange. Inherited property between The Crescent and Barton's Place in Carlisle.
- Died in 1893, aged ~60.

Denis Perriam [records](_Perriam%20articles%20and%20research.md#%22Boags%20made%20buses%20in%20Carlisle%20until%20the%20motor%20engine%20drove%20them%20off%20into%20exile%22%20;%20Past%20and%20Present%20in%20Cumbria,%20by%20Denis%20Perriam;%20Cumberland%20News,%201%20July%202005,%20p8) that: "*When [Mary Barton](Mary%20Irving%20%281804-1890%29.md) died in 1890 she bequeathed to her daughter [Elizabeth](Elizabeth%20Barton%20%281822-1909%29.md) “her house in the Crescent and warehouse adjoining” and to her son **Thomas** the premises behind “where-in the business of Barton and Sons is now carried on,” reported the Journal. But Thomas Barton, the coach builder, did not outlive his mother long because his will was published in the Journal in April 1893. His brother [Robert Barton (1828-1917)](Robert%20Barton%20%281828-1917%29.md) had no interest in the firm so it was offered as a going concern by the executors with a lease on the works.*"

# Will

His will is mentioned in [\*Carlisle Journal 14 April 1893, p5](_Carlisle%20Journal.md#14-april-1893-p5). [Denis Perriam](_Perriam%20articles%20and%20research.md)'s summary of this article reads "*Details of will of **Thomas Barton (1833-1893)**, coachbuilder. £4,447. Will executors his brother [Robert Barton (1829-1917)](Robert%20Barton%20%281828-1917%29.md), J Hewetson Brown & Samuel Boustead sole guardians of his children Harry & [Bowman Barton](Bertie%20Bowman%20Barton%20%28d.1917%29.md). Mentions [Albert Edward Barton (1861-1932)](Albert%20Edward%20Barton%20%281861-1932%29.md), son of his brother [Edward Barton (1828-1913)](Edward%20Barton%20%281828-1913%29.md) of Carnforth.*"

# Children

According to the will extract above:

- Henry (Harry) Barton
- [Bertie Bowman Barton (d.1917)](Bertie%20Bowman%20Barton%20%28d.1917%29.md)
