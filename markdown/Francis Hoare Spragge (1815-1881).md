---
title: "Francis Hoare Spragge (1815-1881)"
---

- Born 18 July 1815 in Blandford, Dorset, son of [Eliza Elliott (1786-1875)](Eliza%20Elliott%20%281786-1875%29.md) and [Rev. Francis Roach Spragge (1782-1838)](Rev.%20Francis%20Roach%20Spragge%20%281782-1838%29.md).
- Married on 16 July 1839, in Streatham, Surrey, [Sarah Prickett (1812-1899)](Sarah%20Prickett%20%281812-1899%29.md).
- Died 4 August 1881 in Paignton, Devon, aged 66.
