---
title: "Rev. George Ruthven Thornton (1845-1895)"
---

- Born 23 March 1845, son of [Rev. Spencer Ruthven Thornton (1813-1850)](Rev.%20Spencer%20Ruthven%20Thornton%20%281813-1850%29.md) and [Caroline Adelaide Dupre (1815-1898)](Caroline%20Adelaide%20Dupre%20%281815-1898%29.md).
- Married (on 12 October 1869 in [Dorking, Surrey](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=dorking,+surrey&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Dorking,+Surrey,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=GqAETeaJCNiAhAfF5uTsBw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CBwQ8gEwAA&z=12)), [Theresa Labouchere (1844-1905)](Theresa%20Labouchere%20%281844-1905%29.md).
- Died 19 June 1895, aged 50.
