title: "Alicia Elliott (1832-1912)"
---

- Born 23 December 1832, first child of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Full name 'Alicia Eling Elliott'.
- Married (on 2 October 1863, in Berne, Switzerland) [Rev. John Brettle Cane (1828-1896)](Rev.%20John%20Brettle%20Cane%20%281828-1896%29.md).
- Died in 1912, aged ~80.
