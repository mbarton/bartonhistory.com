---
title: "Clarissa Squire (1780-1830)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x4 grandmother.
- Daughter of [Joseph Squire](Joseph%20Squire.md) and [Elizabeth Spurrell](Elizabeth%20Spurrell.md).
- Married, on 22 November 1798, [John Dougan (1765-1826)](John%20Dougan%20%281765-1826%29.md).
- Mother of [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md) and [Mary Stephen Dougan (1804-1881)](Mary%20Stephen%20Dougan%20%281804-1881%29.md).
- Died 11 May 1830, of [dropsy](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edema).

Photo of miniature portrait from [Tina Woodhead](Living%20connections.md#tina-woodhead) via [Laurel Rockliff](Living%20connections.md#laurel-rockliff).

# Mentions

In [Emily's Memoir, p7](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#7):\
"*My **Grandmother Dougan** was known in Devon as one of the 3 beautiful Miss Squires; her Christian name was Clarissa. (On 2 separate papers given me by my cousin [Sir Claude Macdonald](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Claude_Maxwell_MacDonald) - great nephew of my Mother's - you will find full particulars of the Dougan family)*."
