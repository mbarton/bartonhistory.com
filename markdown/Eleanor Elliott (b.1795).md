---
title: "Eleanor Elliott (b.1795)"
---

- Born 13 November 1795, daughter of [Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832)](Charles%20Elliott%20the%20cabinet-maker%20%281752-1832%29.md) and [Eling Venn (1758-1843)](Eling%20Venn%20%281758-1843%29.md).
- Married Rev. John Babington (Rector of [Cossington, Leicestershire](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=cossington&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Cossington,+Leicester,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=-gMATaidLMrPhAfdwLijCw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CBoQ8gEwAA&z=12))
- She and her husband lived with [Charlotte Elliott (1789-1871)](Charlotte%20Elliott%20%281789-1871%29.md), according to <u>**The Life and Friendships of Catherine Marsh (1917)** by Lucy Elizabeth Marshall O'Rorke, p243</u>, (see [here](Catherine%20Marsh%20%281818-1912%29.md)).
