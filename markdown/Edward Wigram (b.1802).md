title: "Edward Wigram (b.1802)"

- Born 30 September 1802, son of [Sir Robert Wigram (1744-1830)](Sir%20Robert%20Wigram%20%281744-1830%29.md) and [Eleanor Watts](Eleanor%20Watts.md).
- Married [Catherine Smith](Catherine%20Smith.md).
- Lived at [2 Connaught Place West, Hyde Park](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=2+Connaught+Place+West,+Hyde+Park&ie=UTF8&hl=en&hq=&hnear=2+Connaught+Pl,+Paddington,+Greater+London+W2+2,+United+Kingdom&z=16).
