---
title: "Elizabeth Barton (b.1832)"
---

- Born 20 April 1832, daughter of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Mentioned in Emily's memoir as 'Aunt Bessie'.
- Never married. Lived with her younger sister [Emily Barton (1842-1927)](Emily%20Barton%20%281842-1927%29.md) and guardian [Josephina Rickman (1808-1892)](Josephina%20Rickman%20%281808-1892%29.md).
