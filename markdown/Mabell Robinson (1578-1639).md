- Married to [Sr Barnard Barton (d.1633)](Sr%20Barnard%20Barton%20%28d.1633%29.md)
- May be an ancestor of [John Barton (d.1720)](John%20Barton%20%28d.1720%29.md)?

# Birth

In the [\*Dalston parish records](_Dalston%20parish%20records.md), v1 p186:\
"*1578 Junij 22 Mabella Robinson filia Rob*".

# Marriage

In the [\*Dalston parish records](_Dalston%20parish%20records.md), v1 p110:\
"*1611 July 23 Barnard Barton et Mabell Robinson*".\
Assuming this is the same Mabell Robinson as in the birth record above, this would mean she was 33 when she married (surprisingly late).

# Burial

In the [\*Dalston parish records](_Dalston%20parish%20records.md), v1 p186:\
"*1639 December Mabell the wife of Sr barnard barton buryed the 5th day*".\
Assuming (not unreasonably) that this is the same Mabell and Barnard as in the marriage record above, this would mean she was 61 when she died.

# Children

Also in the [\*Dalston parish records](_Dalston%20parish%20records.md), v1 p186:\
"*1639 October Margrat the daughter of barnard barton buryed the 30th day*".\
Thus her daughter Margrat/**Margaret** died a little over a month before her mother. This is born out by her will (below) which mentions "*all the goods of my daughter margaret Barton Late* *deceased*". The same will also mentions a son **John Barton** (Mabell's executor) and another daughter **Elizabeth** (who married a **David Couper** and had a daughter **Marge**).
