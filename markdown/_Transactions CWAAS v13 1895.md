<u>**Transactions of the Cumberland & Westmorland Antiquarian & Archaeological Society**</u> is a long-standing local history periodical.<u>Volume 13, 1895</u> is available online [here](http://www.archive.org/details/transactionscum05ferggoog). An extract of interest is reproduced below:

# p117

[[Embedded Internet Archive book viewer]](http://www.archive.org/stream/transactionscum05ferggoog?ui=embed#mode/2up/page/n168)

"[From the registers of the parish of Westward]\
