---
title: "Joan Barton (1898-1900)"
---

- Born 27 December 1898, daughter of [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and [Esther Broadbent (1873-1959)](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md).
- Full name 'Esther Joan Barton' but always known as 'Joan'.
- Twin of [Dora Barton (1898-1962)](Dora%20Barton%20%281898-1962%29.md).
- Died 5 September 1900, aged 1½, of typhoid.
