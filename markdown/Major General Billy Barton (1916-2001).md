---
title: "Major General Billy Barton (1916-2001)"
---

- First cousin of [Ronald Barton (1901-1986)](Ronald%20Barton%20%281901-1986%29.md)
- Born January 17 1916, youngest son of [Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941)](Rev.%20Jack%20Barton%20%281866-1941%29.md) and [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md).
- Christened 'Francis Christopher Barton', but nick-named Billy Barton after a [race horse](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_National#Tipperary_Tim).
- Educated at [Haileybury](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haileybury_and_Imperial_Service_College) (the last Barton to go there), Batten House.
- Commissioned into the [Royal Marines](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Marines) in 1934.
- Married (on October 4 1939 in Plymouth) [Olivia Mary Darroll-Smith](Olivia%20Mary%20Darroll-Smith.md) (Molly).
- Kept a fascinating [diary](_Billy%20Barton%20Diary%20and%20Letters.md) throughout World War 2, during which he travelled the oceans mainly on the [HMS Enterprise](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Enterprise_(D52)) and the [HMS Nigeria](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Nigeria).
- Served in [40 Commando](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/40_Commando) in [Malaya](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Malaya).
- Commanded [45 Commando](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/45_Commando) in [Cyprus](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus) (1958-1960).
- Died 2001, aged ~85.
