---
title: "Claude George Thornton (1776-1866)"
---

- Born 20 January 1776, son of [Godfrey Thornton (1737-1805)](Godfrey%20Thornton%20%281737-1805%29.md) and [Jane Codin](Jane%20Codin.md).
- Married (on 18 April 1806 at [St Martin-in-the-Fields](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Martin-in-the-Fields), Westminster) [Frances Anne Smith (1785-1862)](Frances%20Anne%20Smith%20%281785-1862%29.md).
- Died 4 August 1866, aged 90.

# Children

With [Frances Anne Smith (1785-1862)](Frances%20Anne%20Smith%20%281785-1862%29.md):

1. Frances Thornton (b.&d.1806).
2. George Smith Thornton (1808-1867).
3. Sophia Thornton (1811-1827).
4. [Rev. Spencer Ruthven Thornton (1813-1850)](Rev.%20Spencer%20Ruthven%20Thornton%20%281813-1850%29.md).
5. Charles Thornton (1827-1851).
