---
title: "Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941)"
---

- Born 22 September 1866, second son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Full name 'John Bernard Barton' but usually known as 'Jack' (or 'Johnnie' in his mother's [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md)).
- Married, on 31 July 1895 at [St. Nicholas Church, Nottingham](http://www.stnics.org/tempsite/index.php), [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md).
- Died 12 September 1941, aged 74.
