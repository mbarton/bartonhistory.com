title: "*Chronicles of Boileau"
---

<u>**The Chronicles of the Family of Boileau** known as de Castelnau de la Garde et de Sainte-Croix de Boiriac. Digby Whicher Boileau, Thomas Whicher Boileau and Peter Mudie Boileau.</u>

From the frontispiece:\
*"Compiled by DIGBY WHICHER BOILEAU over many years and up to his death at the age of 82, in 1976. Subsequently the work was continued by his brother,* *THOMAS WHICHER BOILEAU, who produced the first version of the book before his own death at the age of 85. This later, and where possible, more complete* *version is now presented by PETER MUDIE BOILEAU, son of Digby and nephew of Tom."*

This unpublished work was passed on to me by Boileau family historian [James Sneyd](http://burningviolin.org/family/index.html), although it is apparent from notes in the [\*NJB family archive](_NJB%20family%20archive.md) that Digby Whicher Boileau had shared an earlier version with [Ronald Barton (1901-1986)](Ronald%20Barton%20%281901-1986%29.md). James Sneyd has declined to make it available online "[in deference to the wishes of the authors](http://burningviolin.org/family/sources.htm)" and - unless I hear different - neither shall I, even though Peter Mudie Boileau, the last living author, died in 2007 (his obituary is [here](http://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/obituaries/military-obituaries/gurkha-obituaries/1555628/Lieutenant-Colonel-Peter-Boileau.html)). It's a shame, but there you go.

The main authors descend from the main Boileau line as follows:

- [Simeon Boileau (1717-1767)](Simeon%20Boileau%20%281717-1767%29.md), father of
- Thomas Boileau (1754-1806), father of
- **Thomas Ebenezer John Boileau (1796-1853)**, father of
- Archibald John Maddy Boileau (1820-1871), father of
- Thomas Smalley Boileau (1851-1933), father of
- Digby Whicher Boileau (1893-1976), father of
- [Peter Mudie Boileau (1926-2007)](http://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/obituaries/military-obituaries/gurkha-obituaries/1555628/Lieutenant-Colonel-Peter-Boileau.html).

This makes Peter Mudie Boileau a 5th cousin of [Ronald Barton (1901-1986)](Ronald%20Barton%20%281901-1986%29.md). James Sneyd, incidentally, is more closely related to the authors, as follows (details from his website at <http://burningviolin.org/family/>):

- **Thomas Ebenezer John Boileau (1796-1853)**, father of
- Ellen Leah Boileau (1838-1918), mother of
- Ellen Octavia Graham (~1866-1955), mother of
- Catherine Charity Graham Bond (1909-1999), grandmother of James Sneyd.
