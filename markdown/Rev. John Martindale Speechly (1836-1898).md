---
title: "Rev. John Martindale Speechly (1836-1898)"
---

- Born 13 November 1836, son of Thomas Kelfull Speechly (1804-1884) and Sarah Bellers (1805-1842).
- Vicar of [Hernehill, Faversham](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=en&q=Hernhill,+Faversham,+Kent,+United+Kingdom&sll=52.556695,-0.130751&sspn=0.23044,0.617294&gl=uk&ie=UTF8&oi=geospell&cd=1&geocode=FV7nDgMd16sOAA&split=0&hq=&hnear=Hernhill,+Faversham,+Kent,+United+Kingdom&z=14).
- Married Mary Gray Grove (b.1836).
- Bishop of [Travancore](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travancore) and [Cochin](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cochin).
- Died 20 January 1898, aged 71. Buried in [Whittlesey, Cambridgeshire](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=whittlesey&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Whittlesey,+Peterborough,+Cambridgeshire,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=dwIBTdT5EoqBhQfz-sjuBw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CBwQ8gEwAA&z=11).

Genealogical information found [here](http://wc.rootsweb.ancestry.com/cgi-bin/igm.cgi?op=GET&db=1171894&id=I765).

Bishop Speechly was a friend of the [Bartons](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) in India, and is mentioned in the [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md).

Bishop Speechly was also (presumably by coincidence) related to Mary Bushby (4th cousin twice removed), who married [Lt. Col. Ted Barton (1897-1971)](Lt.%20Col.%20Ted%20Barton%20%281897-1971%29.md).

Even more curiously, my own wife is a Speechley, whose ancestry traces back to Whittlesey, as does the Bishop's.
