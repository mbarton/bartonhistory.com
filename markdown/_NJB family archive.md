title: "*NJB family archive"
---

[Nicholas J. Barton](Living%20descendants.md#Nicholas Barton) has in his possession a veritable treasure trove of family history documents, including many old photographs, which have been accumulated over at least 4 generations. My goal is to convert as much of this to electronic form as possible (and desirable) and place the contents on this website, but I have only just begun.

All family portraits on this website, <u>if not credited elsewhere</u>, are taken from this archive, but it usually isn't possible to determine their provenance beyond that.
