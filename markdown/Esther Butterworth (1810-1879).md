---
title: "Esther Butterworth (1810-1879)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20descendants.md#David Barton)'s great x3 grandmother.
- Born 12 January 1810, daughter of Benjamin Butterworth.
- Married (on 17 May 1832, in Longwood, Huddersfield), [John Broadbent (1796-1880)](John%20Broadbent%20%281796-1880%29.md).
- Died 11 October 1879, aged 69.
