---
title: "Emily Barton (1842-1927)"
---

- Born 4 November 1842, youngest child of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Died 1927, aged ~85.
- Never married. Lived with her older sister [Elizabeth Barton (b.1832)](Elizabeth%20Barton%20%28b.1832%29.md) and their guardian [Josephina Rickman (1808-1892)](Josephina%20Rickman%20%281808-1892%29.md).
