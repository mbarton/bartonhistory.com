---
title: "Sir Hubert Egerton Young (1885-1950)"
---

- Born 1885, second son of [Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840)](Sir%20William%20Mackworth%20Young%20%28b.1840%29.md) and [Frances May Egerton](Frances%20May%20Egerton.md).
- Married in 1924 Margaret Rose Mary Reynolds, and had three sons with her.
- Governor of Nyasaland (Malawi) 1932-1934.
- Governor of Northern Rhodesia 1935-1938.
- Governor of Trinidad and Tobago 1938-1942.
- Author of "[The Independent Arab](http://books.google.co.uk/books?id=M8Vh0WuoIFkC)".
- His Wikipedia entry is [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Winthrop_Young).
- Died 20 April 1950 in Portugal, aged ~65.
