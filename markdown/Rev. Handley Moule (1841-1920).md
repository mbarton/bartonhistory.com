---
title: "Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920)"
---

- Born 23 December 1841, son of Rev. Henry Moule (1801-1880).
- Full name 'Handley Carr Glyn Moule'.
- A good friend and colleague of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
- Married, on 16 August 1881, John's sister-in-law [Mary Elliott (b.1844)](Mary%20Elliott%20%28b.1844%29.md).
- Became [Bishop of Durham](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishop_of_Durham) (1901-1920), from whence he followed tradition by signing his name 'H. Dunelm' (the Latin for Durham).
- His wikipedia entry is [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handley_Moule).
- Wrote the preface in '[John Barton: a Memoir (1910)](_Barton%201910.md)' by John's son [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md).
- Died 8 May 1920, aged 78.
