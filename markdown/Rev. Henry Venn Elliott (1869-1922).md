- Born 15 January 1869, son of [Sir Charles Alfred Elliott (1835-1911)](Sir%20Charles%20Alfred%20Elliott%20%281835-1911%29.md) and [Louisa Jane Dumbell (1841-1879)](Louisa%20Jane%20Dumbell%20%281841-1879%29.md).
- Known as 'Harry'.
- Married (on 6 October 1896), [Florence Margaret Hunter (b.1873)](Florence%20Margaret%20Hunter%20%28b.1873%29.md).
- Died 26 August 1922, aged 53.
