---
title: "Sir Robert Wigram (1744-1830)"
---

- Born 30 January 1744, son of [John Wigram](John%20Wigram.md) and [Mary Clifford](Mary%20Clifford.md).
- Wikipedia entry [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Robert_Wigram,_1st_Baronet).
- Married firstly (on 19 December 1772 at All Saints, Stamford, Lincoln, England) [Catherine Broadhurst](Catherine%20Broadhurst.md) (or Brodhurst).
- Married secondly (on 23 June 1787 at St. Botolph Aldgate, London, England) [Eleanor Watts](Eleanor%20Watts.md).
- Had 23 children in all!
- Died 6 November 1830, aged 86.

Most names and dates for Robert and his children are taken from www.familysearch.org.
