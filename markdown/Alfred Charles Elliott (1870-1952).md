- Born 20 September 1870, son of [Sir Charles Alfred Elliott (1835-1911)](Sir%20Charles%20Alfred%20Elliott%20%281835-1911%29.md) and [Louisa Jane Dumbell (1841-1879)](Louisa%20Jane%20Dumbell%20%281841-1879%29.md).
- Known as 'Alfy'.
- Married (on 18 December 1894), [Maud Beeching (d.1946)](Maud%20Beeching%20%28d.1946%29.md).
- Died 1952, aged ~82.
