---
title: "Ronald Barton (1901-1986)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s grandfather.
- Born 10 January 1901, middle son of [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and [Esther Broadbent (1873-1959)](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md).
- Full name 'Ronald Cecil Nicholson Barton'.
- Contemporary of [Clark Gable (1901-1960)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Gable) and [Louis Armstrong (1901-1971)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Armstrong).
- Married (on 7 June 1930 in Camberwell, London) [Mary Carty Farrell (1907-1999)](Mary%20Carty%20Farrell%20%281907-1999%29.md).
- In the 1920s he was an actor, mostly at the Old Vic (using stage name of '[Ronald Nicholson](http://theatricalia.com/person/j4z/ronald-nicholson/past)'). He once worked with [Laurence Olivier](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Olivier).
- Became a copy-writer at advertising firm S. H. Benson Ltd, and worked on famous [Guinness](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guiness) adverts (see [here](_Ronald%20Barton%20Diary%20and%20Letters.md#march-5th-wednesday) and [here](http://www.independent.co.uk/news/media/advertising/guinness-a-campaign-thats-been-brewing-for-80-years-1419144.html)).
- His war-time diary covering eight months in 1941 is reproduced [here](_Ronald%20Barton%20Diary%20and%20Letters.md).
- After the war he [wrote a radio adaptation](_Ronald%20Barton%20Diary%20and%20Letters.md#ronald-barton-to-freddy-colquhoun-july-1946) of [G. K. Chesterton](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._K._Chesterton)'s "[The Man Who Was Thursday](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Man_Who_Was_Thursday)" for the BBC which was (re-?)[broadcast](http://www.suttonelms.org.uk/monday-play.html) in 1953 and 1975.
- Father of [Nicholas J. Barton](Living%20connections.md#Nicholas Barton).
- Died 1986.
