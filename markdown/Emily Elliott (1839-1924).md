- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md)'s great x2 grandmother. Descendant of [William the Conqueror](Royal%20ancestry.md), through her [Boileau](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md) (Huguenot) heritage
- Born 7 May 1839, daughter of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Full name 'Emily Eugenia Elliott'. Known to some relatives as 'Millie'.
- Contemporary of [John Butler Yeats (1839-1922)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Butler_Yeats) and [Thomas Hardy (1840-1928)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Hardy).
- Married (on 12 October 1863, in [Ludhiana, India](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludhiana)), [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
- Mother of [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md).
- Died 11 February 1924, aged 84.

Emily described her own life in some detail in a really rather interesting [memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md).
