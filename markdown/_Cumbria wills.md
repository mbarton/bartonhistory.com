<u>**Index to the wills proved in the Consistory court of Carlisle, 1661-1750 (1998)**; published by British Record Society</u> lists the following wills under 'Barton' and 'Burton' (the two names sometimes seem to get confused):\
W = will, W[N] = nuncapative will, A = administration bond (probate), I = inventory

Documents that have been acquired from [\*Cumbria archive service](_Cumbria%20archive%20service.md) are <u>underlined</u> below (transcripts of some, but not yet all, can be found on this site).
