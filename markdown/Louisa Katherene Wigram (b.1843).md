---
title: "Louisa Katherene Wigram (b.1843)"
---

- Born 15 July 1843, youngest daughter of [Edward Wigram (b.1802)](Edward%20Wigram%20%28b.1802%29.md) and [Catherine Smith](Catherine%20Smith.md).

"*That next Christmas vacation* [1857] *I went to spend a few days with the Wigrams at Brighton, where for some years past it had been their habit to spend the four or five winter months. They had taken [82 Marine Parade](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=82+Marine+Parade,+Brighton&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=82+Marine+Parade,+Brighton,+Brighton+and+Hove+BN2+1,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=jPwJTZa-FcOHhQfGuZiFDw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CBkQ8gEwAA&z=16) and it was then I was first introduced not only to [Mr and Mrs. Wigram](Edward%20Wigram%20%28b.1802%29.md) but also to Miss Dennis, commonly known then and since as Denny who had taught [Cathy](Catherine%20Wigram%20%281831-1860%29.md) and [Fred](Rev.%20Fred%20Wigram%20%281834-1897%29.md) their letters in years gone by and was still governess to **Louisa**, the youngest sister then a girl of fifteen, who had her cousin Florence Deedes (now Mrs Saumerer Smith) to share her lessons with her.*" [\*Memoirs of John Barton](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md)
