---
title: "Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875)"
---

- Born 25 July 1793, son of [Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832)](Charles%20Elliott%20the%20cabinet-maker%20%281752-1832%29.md) and [Eling Venn (1758-1843)](Eling%20Venn%20%281758-1843%29.md).
- Half-brother of [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md).
- In Wikipedia [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Bishop_Elliott).
- Author of "Horae Apocalypticae, A commentary on the Apocalypse, critical and historical" (see Wikipedia entry [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horae_Apocalypticae)).
- Died in 1875, aged ~82.
