---
title: "Eling Venn (1758-1843)"
---

- Born 7 February 1758, daughter of [Henry Venn (1725-1797)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Venn_(Clapham_Sect)).
- Married, on 2 December 1785, widower [Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832)](Charles%20Elliott%20the%20cabinet-maker%20%281752-1832%29.md).
- Mother of [Charlotte Elliott (1789-1871)](Charlotte%20Elliott%20%281789-1871%29.md) and [Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875)](Rev.%20Edward%20Bishop%20Elliott%20%281793-1875%29.md).
- Great aunt of [John Venn (1834-1923)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Venn) the mathematician and inventor of Venn diagrams.
- Died 17 April 1843, aged 85.
