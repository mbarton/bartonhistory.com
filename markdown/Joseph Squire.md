title: "Joseph Squire"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20descendants.md#David%20Barton)'s great x5 grandfather.
- Wealthy merchant in Plymouth.
- Married firstly Ann Dunning Jago, with whom he had two children.
- Married secondly Elizabeth Spurrell.
- Parent, with Elizabeth, of [Clarissa Squire (1780-1830)](Clarissa%20Squire%20%281780-1830%29.md).

# Rowe connection?

Interestingly, my ([DBHB](Living%20descendants.md#David%20Barton)) mother's paternal ancestry (a Devonian family named Rowe) *also* married into a Squire family. Her great x3 grandfather John Rowe (b.1751) married Ann Squire (d.1788) on 1 November 1771, in [Okehampton](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=Okehampton&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Okehampton,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ll=50.739932,-3.999023&spn=0.029983,0.077162&z=14). The Squire name was handed down as a middle name through several generations of their descendants. It has proved difficult to learn more, since so many records were destroyed in Exeter during the war.
