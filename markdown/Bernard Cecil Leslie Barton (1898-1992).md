---
title: "Bernard Cecil Leslie Barton (1898-1992)"
---

- Born 9 September 1898, second son of [Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941)](Rev.%20Jack%20Barton%20%281866-1941%29.md) and [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md).
- Married, in July 1928, 'Nancy' [Agnes Thomson (1900-1973)](Agnes%20Thomson%20%281900-1973%29.md).
- Became Chairman of Bluecoat's School, Nottingham.
- Died 22 November 1992, aged 94.
