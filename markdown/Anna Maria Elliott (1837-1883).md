---
title: "Anna Maria Elliott (1837-1883)"
---

- Born 3 September 1837, daughter of [Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875)](Rev.%20Edward%20Bishop%20Elliott%20%281793-1875%29.md) and [Harriet Emily Steele](Harriet%20Emily%20Steele.md).
- Married (on 12 October 1875) [Rev. Robert Douglas Monro](Rev.%20Robert%20Douglas%20Monro.md).
- Died 1883, aged ~46.

# Children

With [Rev. Robert Douglas Monro](Rev.%20Robert%20Douglas%20Monro.md):

1. Annie Florence Monro (b.1876)
2. Edith Elliott Monro (b.1878)
3. Robert Elliott Monro (b.1878)
4. Kathleen Elliott Monro (b.1882)
