---
title: "Margaret Susan Monro (1898-1987)"
---

- Born 19 May 1898, eldest daughter of [Isabel Mary Young (b.1870)](Isabel%20Mary%20Young%20%28b.1870%29.md) and [Archibald Vere Monro](Archibald%20Vere%20Monro.md).
- Known as 'Meg'.
- Married 'Jimmy' D. H. James.
- Died 22 September 1987, at Crowborough, Sussex.
