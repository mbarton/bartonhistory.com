---
title: "Rev. Fred Wigram (1834-1897)"
---

- Born 1 June 1834, son of [Edward Wigram (b.1802)](Edward%20Wigram%20%28b.1802%29.md) and [Catherine Smith](Catherine%20Smith.md).
- Good friend of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) who married Fred's [sister](Catherine%20Wigram%20%281831-1860%29.md). Mentioned repeatedly in [John's memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md).
- Married (on 31 May 1859, at Osmaston near Ashbourne, Derbyshire), [Frances Wright (1840-1914)](Frances%20Wright%20%281840-1914%29.md).
- Died 10 March 1897, aged 62.

More info on Fred and his family can be found at <http://www.highfieldhistory.co.uk/#/rev-frederic-edward-wigram/4537869766> and also at <http://www.thepeerage.com/p46075.htm#i460744>.
