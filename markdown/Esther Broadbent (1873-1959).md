title: "Esther Broadbent (1873-1959)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md)'s great grandmother.
- Born 5 November 1873, daughter of [Col. John Edward Broadbent (1845-1931)](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) and [Dora Nicholson (1844-1897)](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md).
- Christened 'Esther Mary Broadbent'.
- Grew up at least partly in [Shimla, India](Shimla%2C%20India.md).
- Married (on 21 October 1896 at [Murree, now in Pakistan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murree)), [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md).
- Mother of [Ronald Barton (1901-1986)](Ronald%20Barton%20%281901-1986%29.md).
- Died 27 November 1959, aged 86.

# Mentions in [Emily's Memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md)

*"In September this year* [1894] *our [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) was very happily engaged at [Simla](Shimla%2C%20India.md) to the daughter of a very old friend,* Esther Broadbent, child of [Colonel Broadbent](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) and of his wife, formerly [Dora Nicholson](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) whom we had known intimately in Calcutta between '65 and '70. Esther and her mother came home in November, and Esther soon found a place in our hearts after we met her at her uncle Sir William Broadbent's house in London." [p108](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#108)

*"**On October 21* [1896] *Cecil and **Esther** were married at [Murree](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murree), in India, by good [Bishop Matthews of Lahore](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_James_Matthew). [Arthur](Arthur%20Elliott%20Barton%20%281864-1931%29.md) was alas! the only one to represent the family, and he sent us a delightful account of that happy and festive occasion. [Colonel](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) & [Mrs. Broadbent](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) grudged nothing to their sweet and only daughter - and the Mother (our friend [Dora Nicholson](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) of early Calcutta days) quite accepted Cecil as a son, during the eight months she lived after this marriage - after which a sudden illness called her 'Home'\*. After a brief honeymoon this young couple settled down in Multan to work there for two years.*\
*(\*In June, 1897. Two months later [Colonel Broadbent](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) having gone to the Malakand Campaign as Chief Engineer, Esther travelled to Barnes Court, Simla, residence of 'Uncle [Willy' Mackworth Young](Sir%20William%20Mackworth%20Young%20%28b.1840%29.md), then Lieutenant-Governor of the Punjab, where [Ted](Lt.%20Col.%20Ted%20Barton%20%281897-1971%29.md) was born, on September 6th.)**"* [p112](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#112)

"*When autumn came we began to look forward to the return of [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and his **wife** and son [Ted](Lt.%20Col.%20Ted%20Barton%20%281897-1971%29.md) - and were thankful when they safely arrived on Sunday evening November 13th. Cecil's calm and Esther's pluck carried them thro a long and anxious journey; and after six weeks quiet rest, dear Esther gave us twin-granddaughters, on December 27, who were christened in February '99, and called '[Dora](Dora%20Barton%20%281898-1962%29.md) and Joan'. They were a great joy to their dear **Mother** and me, tho' of course a great handful; and we were rejoiced to give them a home* *for three months, especially as Esther's [own dear mother](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md) was gone aloft, and [Colonel Broadbent](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) had not then retired from Indian life, so they had no Home in England this year but ours.*" [pp113-114](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#113)
