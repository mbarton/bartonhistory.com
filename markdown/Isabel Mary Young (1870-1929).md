title: "Isabel Mary Young (1870-1929)"

- Born 7 May 1870, only daughter of [Isabel Maria Elliott (1842-1870)](Isabel%20Maria%20Elliott%20%281842-1870%29.md) and [Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840)](Sir%20William%20Mackworth%20Young%20%28b.1840%29.md).
- Married (on 21 April 1897, at Government House, [Lahore](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore)) [Archibald Vere Monro](Archibald%20Vere%20Monro.md) (1867-1944).
- Died 1929
