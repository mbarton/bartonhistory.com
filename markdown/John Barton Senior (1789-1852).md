---
title: "John Barton Senior (1789-1852)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x3 grandfather.
- Born 4 June 1789 (after his father's death), youngest son of [John Barton the Elder (1754-1789)](John%20Barton%20the%20Elder%20%281754-1789%29.md) and [Elizabeth Horne (1760-1833)](Elizabeth%20Horne%20%281760-1833%29.md).
- Contemporary of [Robert Peel (1788-1850)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Peel) and [Lord Byron (1788-1824)](Famous%20connections.md#Lord%20Byron).
- Half-brother to [Maria Hack (1777-1844)](Maria%20Hack%20%281777-1844%29.md) and [Bernard Barton the Quaker poet (1784-1849)](Bernard%20Barton%20the%20Quaker%20poet%20%281784-1849%29.md).
- Moved to Chichester, in Sussex.
- Principally known as an early economist. His work was discussed by [Karl Marx](Famous%20connections.md#Karl%20Marx) in [Theories of Surplus Value, a.k.a. Das Kapital v4 ch18](_Marx%201861-3.md).
- Married firstly (in Wandsworth, London, on 29 August 1811) [Ann Woodrouffe Smith (1791-1821)](Ann%20Woodrouffe%20Smith%20%281791-1821%29.md).
- Married secondly (at [Smeeth Hill House](http://www.britishlistedbuildings.co.uk/en-181709-smeeth-hill-house-smeeth), near Ashford in Kent, on 23 July 1828) [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Father of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
- Wrote a [sweet letter](_Writings%20of%20John%20Barton%20Senior.md#letter-from-john-barton-senior-to-his-children-april-1850) to his children in 1850.
- Died 10 March 1852, aged 62.

A [lengthy biography](_Barton%201980s.md) was produced in the 1980s (but not published) by his great grandson Major Robert G.L. Barton.\
An excellent short biography of John Barton Senior is given in [\*Griffiths 2001-2](_Griffiths%202001-2.md). His life is also briefly discussed on the Bloomsbury People blog [here](http://bloomsburypeople.blogspot.com/2009/10/barton-family-abolition-of-slavery-and.html).

# First Marriage

The following is Public Record Office reference RG 6/541:\
![john barton anne woodrouffe smith 1811 SMALL.jpg](../files/john%20barton%20anne%20woodrouffe%20smith%201811%20SMALL.jpg "john barton anne woodrouffe smith 1811 SMALL.jpg")

"*On the <u>twenty ninth</u> Day of the <u>eighth</u> Month, One Thousand <u>Eight</u> Hundred and <u>eleven</u>... <u>John Barton</u> of <u>Chichester in the County of Sussex, Merchant</u>.... Son of <u>John Barton, late</u>.... of <u>Hertford deceased</u>.... and <u>Elizabeth</u>.... his Wife, and <u>Ann Woodrouffe Smith</u>, Daughter of <u>Thomas Woofrouffe Smith</u> of <u>Stockwell in the County of Surry deceased</u>.... and <u>Ann</u>... his Wife, took each other in Marriage in a publick Assembly of the people called Quakers, in <u>their meeting house at Wandsworth in the County of Surry</u> in the presence of us*

<u>*Tho.s Reynolds of Wallington, Surry, Bleacher*</u>\
<u>*Geo. Harrison, Wandsworth*</u>\
<u>*Sam.l West of Billiter Lane, London, Warehousekeeper*</u>

*This Marriage was solemnized between us,*\
<u>*John Barton*</u>\
<u>*Ann Woodrouffe Smith*</u>"

# Birkbeck College

John was a founder of Birkbeck College when it was called the London Mechanics' Institution. The institute has moved from its original location, but the stone bearing John's name has been moved with it (currently on the ground floor behind the lobby, facing the lifts):\
