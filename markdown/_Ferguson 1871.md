[This is strikingly wrong: [Bernard Barton of Carlisle (1728-1773)](Bernard%20Barton%20of%20Carlisle%20%281728-1773%29.md) was surely **not** a Quaker, married and buried as he was in CoE churches. His son [John](John%20Barton%20the%20Elder%20%281754-1789%29.md), on the other hand, converted to Quakerism, perhaps to woo Maria Done.]
