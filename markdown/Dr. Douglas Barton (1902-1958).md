- Born 29 January 1902, youngest son of [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and [Esther Broadbent (1873-1959)](Esther%20Broadbent%20%281873-1959%29.md).
- Married firstly Mary Worthington, secondly Jean Fletcher.
- Died 24 October 1958, aged 56.
