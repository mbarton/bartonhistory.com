title: "Albert Edward Barton (1861-1932)"

- Son of [Edward Barton (1828-1913)](Edward%20Barton%20%281828-1913%29.md) and [Mary Anne Carter (1831-1911)](Mary%20Anne%20Carter%20%281831-1911%29.md)
- Married firstly Margaret.
- Moved to America.
- Married secondly [Marguerite Norma Smith (1884-1979)](Marguerite%20Norma%20Smith%20%281884-1979%29.md).

I'm indebted to Richard Hargreaves, a grandson of Albert by his first wife, for sharing his information on this branch with me, as well as Martin Barton and Paul Betteley.

# Children

With Margaret:

1. [Robert Barton (1893-1916)](Robert%20Barton%20%281893-1916%29.md)
2. Mary Barton (b.1895), who in 1915 married James Hargreaves.
3. Margaret Davies Barton (b.1899), who in 1919 married Thomas Tuckniss Morris
4. Bessie Barton, who married ???? Watson.

- two more children.
