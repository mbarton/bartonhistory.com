- Born 5 November 1798, son of [Pascoe Grenfell (1761-1838)](Pascoe%20Grenfell%20%281761-1838%29.md) and [Georgiana St. Leger (d.1818)](Georgiana%20St.%20Leger%20%28d.1818%29.md).
- Married [Catherine Anne Dupre (b.1804)](Catherine%20Anne%20Dupre%20%28b.1804%29.md).
- Died 23 August 1874, aged 75.

These details provided by descendant [Malcolm Barton](Living%20descendants.md#Malcolm Barton). But see also <http://www.thepeerage.com/p7075.htm#i70741> which gives some different details, including date of death 28 March 1879, and a second wife called [Penelope Frances Madan (d.1868)](Penelope%20Frances%20Madan%20%28d.1868%29.md).
