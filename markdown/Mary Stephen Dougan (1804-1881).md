---
title: "Mary Stephen Dougan (1804-1881)"
---

- Born 21 July 1804, daughter of [John Dougan (1765-1826)](John%20Dougan%20%281765-1826%29.md) and [Clarissa Squire (1780-1830)](Clarissa%20Squire%20%281780-1830%29.md).
- Sister of [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md) and aunt of [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- In 1826, completed and sent a [government report on slavery in the West Indies](_Dougan%201826.md) written by her father before his death.
- A letter to her from her father's old friend [James Stephen](Famous%20connections.md#james-stephen-1758-1832-and-sir-james-stephen-1789-1859) can be read [here](_Correspondence%20of%20Mary%20Stephen%20Dougan.md).
- Married (on 11 March 1833 at [St Pancras Old Church, London](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Pancras_Old_Church)) [Richard Walkden (d.1880)](Richard%20Walkden%20%28d.1880%29.md) (his second marriage).
- Emigrated to Australia on board the first Australian voyage of the [S.S. Great Britain](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Great_Britain) in 1852.
- Later travelled to New Zealand to stay with her daughter Emily (who married Charles Strickland Mackie).
- Died 18 January 1881, aged 76/77. Buried in [Barrhill](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=en&geocode=&q=Barrhill,+NZ&aq=&sll=-43.6711,171.855011&sspn=0.0632,0.154324&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Barrhill,+Canterbury,+New+Zealand&z=12) cemetery, New Zealand.

Mary's life was fascinating, impressive and a little bit tragic. She was born into a wealthy plantation-owning family that lost everything in its opposition to slavery. She completed her [father](John%20Dougan%20%281765-1826%29.md)'s [abolitionist work](_Dougan%201826.md) after his death. Then she emigrated to Australia on the first antipodean voyage of the [S.S. Great Britain](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Great_Britain) in 1852. She ended up buried in a very modest grave in New Zealand.

Her middle name surely derives from her father's close friendship with the [Stephen family](Famous%20connections.md#James%20Stephen). The [Wilberforce](Famous%20connections.md#William%20Wilberforce) and [Venn](Eling%20Venn%20%281758-1843%29.md) families also had close ties to the Stephen family, and these names also crop up in descendants of [John Dougan](John%20Dougan%20%281765-1826%29.md).

# S.S. Great Britain

 [Brunel](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isambard_Kingdom_Brunel)'s famous iron steam ship the [S.S. Great Britain](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SS_Great_Britain) (now refurbished and displayed in Bristol, UK) made its maiden voyage to New York in 1845. Its first voyage to Australia was in 1852. According to researcher Dorothy Wettenhall (see [here](http://mepnab.netau.net/s/s21.html)), the passenger manifests, published in "[Is Yours an S. S. "Great Britain" Family?](http://www.amazon.co.uk/Yours-S-Great-Britain-Family/dp/0859373428)", list the following Walkdens as being on this voyage: "*Basil, Cecil, Clarence, Francis, Frederick, George, John, Emily, Mr. Richard Walkden, **Mrs. Walkden**, Miss Walkden* [the latter being Mary Ann?]".
