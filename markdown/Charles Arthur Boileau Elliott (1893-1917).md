---
title: "Charles Arthur Boileau Elliott (1893-1917)"
---

- Born 9 February 1893, eldest son of [Charles Lister Boileau Elliott (1864-1940)](Charles%20Lister%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281864-1940%29.md) and Katherine Peel O'Brien
- Lived in London.
- Served with 4th Battalion [Royal Norfolk Regiment](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norfolk_Regiment), Gallipoli 1915 & Egypt 1916, then as a Second Lieutenant in 1st Battalion [Somerset Light Infantry](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset_Light_Infantry).
- Died of wounds sustained in the [Battle of Arras](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Arras_(1917)) , on 12 April 1917, aged 24. Buried at [Duisans British Cemetery](http://twgpp.org/information.php?id=2671313) near [Arras](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arras,_France).

# Pocket Watch

A pocket watch exists with the following engraving:

*C.A.B.ELLIOTT*\
*FROM HIS DEAR*\
*GREAT GRANDFATHER*\
*April 1901*\
*\**

Only two of his great grandfathers have been identified: [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) who died to early, and John Samuel Lister, father of [Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843)](Hannah%20Frances%20Lister%20%28b.1843%29.md). Who gave Charles the watch?

# Gravestone

At the [Duisans British Cemetery](http://twgpp.org/information.php?id=2671313) near [Arras](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arras,_France). This image of it courtesy of [Charles Baron](Living%20connections.md#charles-baron):\
![Charles Arthur Boileau Elliott gravestone at Duisans SMALL.JPG](../files/Charles%20Arthur%20Boileau%20Elliott%20gravestone%20at%20Duisans%20SMALL.JPG "Charles Arthur Boileau Elliott gravestone at Duisans SMALL.JPG")\
The stone carries the crest of the [Somerset Light Infantry 13th Regiment of Foot](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset_Light_Infantry) (which was garrisoned at the "[Jellalabad Barracks](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset_Light_Infantry#Jellalabad_Barracks)" in Taunton). The inscription reads:

"*SECOND LIEUTENANT C. A. B. ELLIOTT, SOMERSET LIGHT INFANTRY, 12TH APRIL 1917, AGE 24.*\
*TRANSLATED FROM THE WARFARE OF THE WORLD INTO THE PEACE OF GOD.*"

# Memorial

In [St. Mary's Church, Tattingstone](St.%20Mary%27s%20Church%2C%20Tattingstone.md) (where his [great grandfather](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) had been vicar), there is a memorial to Charles, reading:\
*"He died the Noblest death a man may die,*\
*Fighting for God & Right & Liberty*\
*And such a death is Immortality."* [From ['To You Who Have Lost' by John Oxenham](http://www.readbookonline.net/readOnLine/47950/)]\
*IN EVER LOVING MEMORY OF CHARLES ARTHUR BOILEAU ELLIOTT, 2nd LIEUT. 1st SOMERSET L.I. DIED OF WOUNDS IN FRANCE APRIL 12, 1917, AGED 24. THE GIFT OF GOD IS ETERNAL LIFE.*\
*"Death cannot long divide*\
*For is it not as if the rose had climbed*\
*My garden wall & blossomed on the other side"* [From ['An April Rhyme' by Alice Carey](http://news.google.com/newspapers?id=fuUmAAAAIBAJ&sjid=wwIGAAAAIBAJ&pg=4559,772268)]\
