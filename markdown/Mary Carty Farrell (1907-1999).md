---
title: "Mary Carty Farrell (1907-1999)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s grandmother.
- Born 2 August 1907, only child of [George Farrell (1872-1956)](George%20Farrell%20%281872-1956%29.md) and [Sarah Carty (1874-1937)](Sarah%20Carty%20%281874-1937%29.md).
- Always known as 'Molly'.
- Her father left his family when Molly was young. He went abroad.
- Married (on 7 June 1930 in Camberwell, London) [Ronald Barton (1901-1986)](Ronald%20Barton%20%281901-1986%29.md).
- Mother of [Nicholas J. Barton](Living%20connections.md#Nicholas Barton).
- Died 8 September 1999, aged 92.
