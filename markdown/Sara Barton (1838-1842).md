---
title: "Sara Barton (1838-1842)"
---

- Born 1838, daughter of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Some dispute about her name. Her father called her 'Sara' in a [letter](_Writings%20of%20John%20Barton%20Senior.md#letter-from-john-barton-senior-to-his-children-april-1850) he wrote, but her brother John spelled it 'Sarah' in his [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md). It is assumed the former is correct.
- Died 9 November 1842, aged ~4, of [scarlet fever](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlet_fever) which also killed her [mother](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md) five days later.
