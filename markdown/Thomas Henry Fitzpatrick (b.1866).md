---
title: "Thomas Henry Fitzpatrick (b.1866)"
---

- Born 2 August 1866, only son of [Anne Barton (1831-1921)](Anne%20Barton%20%281831-1921%29.md) and [Rev. Thomas Henry Fitzpatrick (d.1866)](Rev.%20Thomas%20Henry%20Fitzpatrick%20%28d.1866%29.md).
- Known as 'Henry'.

"*During the first half of 1872 I had to hunt for a home in which I could happily leave you four Boys. I corresponded with, and saw twelve families before I decided finally to leave [Arthur](Arthur%20Elliott%20Barton%20%281864-1931%29.md) and [Jack](Rev.%20Jack%20Barton%20%281866-1941%29.md) at Harrow, with Mr. and Mrs. James Stuart; and [Fred](Fred%20Barton%20%281868-1929%29.md) and [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) with Aunt [Annie](Anne%20Barton%20%281831-1921%29.md) and **Henry**, then 6 years old, at [Dorking](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=dorking&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Dorking,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=yb38TJ3MJYWShAfI9ZTZCg&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CBoQ8gEwAA&ll=51.232688,-0.32959&spn=0.059333,0.154324&z=13). Both homes were very comfortable - though the Stuarts was very expensive; but we had every reason to believe that parents who had succeeded so well in bringing up their own 4 boys, would do their duty well by ours.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p65](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#65).
