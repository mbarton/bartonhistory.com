title: "*ABH research notes"
---

In November 2010, Andrew B. Humphries, author of [Twelve Bobbins Spinning](_Humphries%201984.md) who lives in [Ivegill](Ivegill.md), Cumbria, donated to me all his research notes and several wonderful family history artifacts. The notes include wills, correspondence, newspaper clippings, book extracts and more.
