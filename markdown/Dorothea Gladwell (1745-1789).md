---
title: "Dorothea Gladwell (1745-1789)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x5 grandmother.
- Born 1745 in Dublin, Ireland, daughter of Francis Gladwell.
- Married, on 3 May 1766, [Solomon Boileau (1745-1810)](Solomon%20Boileau%20%281745-1810%29.md).
- Died 4 May 1789 in Dublin, Ireland, aged 44.

# Gradwell or Gladwell?

Solomon and Dorothy are listed in [ThePeerage.com](http://www.thepeerage.com/p17173.htm#i171723) . This gives her name as "[Dorothea Gradwell](http://www.thepeerage.com/p17173.htm#i171724)". [James Sneyd](http://burningviolin.org/family/index) gives her name as "[Dorothy Gladwell](http://burningviolin.org/family/Web%20Cards/ps06/ps06_192.htm)". The family history website at [www.holmesacourt.org](http://www.holmesacourt.org/hac/3/8887.htm) (which also gives her dates as shown here) gives her name as "**Dorothea Gladwell**", and this is presumed the correct spelling (for now!)
