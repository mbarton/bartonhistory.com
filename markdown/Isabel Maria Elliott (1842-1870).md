---
title: "Isabel Maria Elliott (1842-1870)"
---

- Born 9 March 1842, daughter of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Sister of [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md), who refers to her as 'Aunt Isa' in her [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md).
- Married, on 17 August 1869, [Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840)](Sir%20William%20Mackworth%20Young%20%28b.1840%29.md).
- Mother of Isabel Mary Young (b.1870).
- Died young, 28 May 1870, aged 28. Her husband remarried in 1881.
