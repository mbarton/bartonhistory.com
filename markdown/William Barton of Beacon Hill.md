- Baptised 1747?, son of John and Margaret Barton of Beaconside??
- Married Isabella Mondeville?
- Lived at Beacon Hill Farm just north of [Ivegill](Ivegill.md)
- Also owned land opposite Broadfield House?
- Unknown relationship to other Ivegill Bartons

# Possible baptism?

The [\*High Head baptismal register](_High%20Head%20baptismal%20register.md) contains the following entry:

*"Anno 1747"*\
*"June 17 Was baptized **William** Son of John and Margaret Barton of **Beaconside**"*

This *could* be the same man who lived at Beacon Hill Farm (see below) and had several children whose baptisms are in the same register (see below). He would have been 32 when he had his first child (Mary), and 53 when he had his last (Robert).

# Children

The [\*High Head baptismal register](_High%20Head%20baptismal%20register.md) contains the following:

"*1779 - November 21 - Mary daughter of **William** **Barton** and Bella his wife of Beacon baptised*\
*1784 - August 21 - was John son of **William** and Bella his wife of Beacon baptised*\
*1788 - May 19th Joseph son of **William Barton** and Isabella his wife, late Mondeville, of Beacon Hill baptised*\
*1790 - September 26 - Margaret, daughter of **William Barton** and his wife, late Mondeville, of Beacon Hill, baptised*\
*1794 - September 15 William, son of **William** of Ivegill, farmer & Isabella his wife (late Mondeville) baptised*\
*1801 - April 22 - Robert, son of **William Barton** of Ivegill, farmer, and Isabella his wife (late Mondeville) baptised*"

The [\*Dalston parish records](_Dalston%20parish%20records.md) contain the following:\
"*1793 / August 24 Margaret, daughter of **Wm: Barton of Beacon hill** aged 3 years buried*" (v2, p289).

Therefore...

With [Isabella Mondeville](Isabella%20Mondeville.md):

1. Mary Barton (b.1779?)
2. John Barton (b.1784?)
3. Joseph Barton (b.1788?)
4. Margaret Barton (1790-1793)
5. William Barton (b.1794?)
6. Robert Barton (b.1801?)

# Relationship to other Ivegill Bartons

We don't know how this William relates to the other Bartons in the area. If he is the son of John and Margaret Barton of Beaconside, who are they? Descendants of [Barnard Barton (d.1715)](Barnard%20Barton%20%28d.1715%29.md)?

The William Barton who owned Beacon Hill is certainly too early to be [William Barton (1785-1846)](William%20Barton%20%281785-1846%29.md) of the coach-building line, and too late to be that man's grandfather [William Barton (1700-1763)](William%20Barton%20%281700-1763%29.md), or even the William Barton who [signs the 1763 probate of that William's will](William%20Barton%20%281700-1763%29.md#notes-on-the-will). Nor does he seem likely to have been [William Barton (1737-1818)](William%20Barton%20%281737-1818%29.md).

With respect to my line, [Bernard Barton of Carlisle (1728-1773)](Bernard%20Barton%20of%20Carlisle%20%281728-1773%29.md) did have a son called William born in 1764 (about the right time), but this infant [died](Bernard%20Barton%20of%20Carlisle%20%281728-1773%29.md#children) almost immediately.
