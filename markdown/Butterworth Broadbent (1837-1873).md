---
title: "Butterworth Broadbent (1837-1873)"
---

- Born in January 1837, son of [John Broadbent (1796-1880)](John%20Broadbent%20%281796-1880%29.md) and [Esther Butterworth (1810-1879)](Esther%20Butterworth%20%281810-1879%29.md).
- Married (in 1862 at St John's Church, Holmfirth) [Julia Minerva Harpin](Julia%20Minerva%20Harpin.md).
- Died in 1873, aged ~36.
