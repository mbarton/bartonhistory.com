title: "Rev. Raymond Henry Barton (1905-1975)"
---

- Born 15 January 1905, son of [Rev. Jack Barton (1866-1941)](Rev.%20Jack%20Barton%20%281866-1941%29.md) and [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md).
- Mentioned, as 'Ray', in the memoir of his sister [Joy Barton (1907-1984)](Joy%20Barton%20%281907-1984%29.md).
- Married [Cecily Fleming](Cecily%20Fleming.md).
- Died 1975, aged ~70.
