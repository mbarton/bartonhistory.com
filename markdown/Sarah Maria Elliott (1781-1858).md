---
title: "Sarah Maria Elliott (1781-1858)"
---

- Born 27 September 1781, daughter of [Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832)](Charles%20Elliott%20the%20cabinet-maker%20%281752-1832%29.md) and Sarah Ann Sherman (d.1784).
- Married (on 13 September 1803), [Rev. Hugh Nicholas Pearson (1776-1856)](Rev.%20Hugh%20Nicholas%20Pearson%20%281776-1856%29.md).
- Her daughter Catherine married Sarah's nephew [William Henry Elliott (1811-1870)](William%20Henry%20Elliott%20%281811-1870%29.md).
- Died in 1858, aged ~77.
