---
title: "*Barton parish registers"
---

Parish registers for [Barton](Barton%20%28hamlet%20%26%20parish%29.md) were published in <u>**The registers of the parish church of Barton, Westmorland. Baptisms and marriages. 1666-1812. Burials, 1666-1830 (1917)** by Henry Brierley</u> (online [here](http://www.archive.org/details/registersofparis00bart)), and contain only three people named Barton, plus one who has Barton as his middle name:

"***An** w. of **Tho: Barton***" 8 November 1684 [Burials p71]\
"***Anne Barton** of Sockbridge Mill, Spinster*" 27 April 1717 [Burials p78]\
"***George Barton** s. of Eliz: **Todd** Widow (late Scholough)*" 25 February 1791 [Baptisms p49]
