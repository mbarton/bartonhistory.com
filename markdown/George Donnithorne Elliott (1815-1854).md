---
title: "George Donnithorne Elliott (1815-1854)"
---

- Born 16 December 1815, youngest son of [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md) and [Alicia Boileau (1779-1851)](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md).
- Died 29 July 1854, aged 38, drowned in [Lake Nainital](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nainital) in India.

"*My father's youngest brother 'George' entered the Indian Army - and was drowned in the deep mountain [Lake of Naini Tal](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nainital) when out shooting in 1850.*" [\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p5](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#5) (Emily gets the date wrong here).
