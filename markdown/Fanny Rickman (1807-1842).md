---
title: "Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x3 grandmother.
- Born ~1807, daughter of Quakers Joseph Rickman (d.1808) and Sarah Rickman, who were second cousins.
- Full name 'Frances Rickman' but always known as 'Fanny'.
- Married (at [Smeeth Hill House](http://www.britishlistedbuildings.co.uk/en-181709-smeeth-hill-house-smeeth), near Ashford in Kent, on 23 July 1828) [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md).
- Mother of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md).
- Died 14 November 1842, aged ~35.
- Her sister [Josephina Rickman (1808-1892)](Josephina%20Rickman%20%281808-1892%29.md) became a foster mother to her children, and in fact became sole guardian after their father also died ten years later.
