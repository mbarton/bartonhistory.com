---
title: "John Campbell Barton (1858-1889)"
---

- Born 30 November 1858, son of [Rev. Joseph Barton (1829-1905)](Rev.%20Joseph%20Barton%20%281829-1905%29.md) and [Ellinor Taylor (1832-1862)](Ellinor%20Taylor%20%281832-1862%29.md).
- Known (at least in [Emily's memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md)) as 'Cam'.
- Died 20 August 1889, aged 30, in [Sierra Leone](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sierra_Leone), of [Blackwater fever](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackwater_fever) ([Emily's memoir p93](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md)).
