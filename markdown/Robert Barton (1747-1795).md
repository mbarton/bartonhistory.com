title: "Robert Barton (1747-1795)"
---

- Son of [William Barton (1700-1763)](William%20Barton%20%281700-1763%29.md) and Frances Setree? According to Richard Hargreaves.
- Son of Robert Barton of Ivegill (b.~1700) and Mary Graham? According to [\*Maupin 1980](_Maupin%201980.md).
- Married, on 10 February 1777, [Mary Richardson (1746-1821)](Mary%20Richardson%20%281746-1821%29.md).
- Died 24 November 1795, aged 48. Buried in Wigton churchyard.

# Oulton Hall

# Other property

According to [\*Penfold](_Penfold.md), Robert's grandson "*inherited on the death of his [father](William%20Barton%20%281785-1846%29.md)* [in 1846] *[Thomas Close](Bartons%20Farm%2C%20Thomas%20Close.md) - which his **grandfather** had purchased & left to his [Second Son - Robert's father](William%20Barton%20%281785-1846%29.md) - the elder son inheriting the [Ivegill](Ivegill.md) Estate."*

The only children currently known are given below, and there William is the only son, so clearly something is missing from our knowledge of this family.

If Robert owned both Thomas Close and Ivegill properties on his death in 1795, he may have bought one or both of them from [John Barton the Elder (1754-1789)](John%20Barton%20the%20Elder%20%281754-1789%29.md), who moved down to London in ~1783.

# Grave

According to [Denis Perriam](_Perriam%20articles%20and%20research.md), the stone in Wigton churchyard, which carries a crest, reads:

"*Sacred to the memory of **Robert Barton** of Wigton, who died Nov 24 1795 aged 48 years. Also Mary his wife, who died August 19 1821 aged 70 years*" [70, not 75 as she would be if born in 1746. Which birth year is correct?]

The same stone also commemorates "*[William Barton](William%20Barton%20%281785-1846%29.md) of Carlisle, their son who died Octr 22 1846 aged 61 years*" and names some of the children of William and [Mary](Mary%20Irving%20%281804-1890%29.md) up to 1852.
