---
title: "Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843)"
---

- Born 16 May 1843, daughter of John Samuel Lister.
- Married (on 21 May 1862), [Charles Pearson Elliott (1833-1876)](Charles%20Pearson%20Elliott%20%281833-1876%29.md).
- Known in [Emily's memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md) as 'Aunt Fannie'.
- Married secondly (on 5 June 1878) Rev. Francis Littler.

A Mr. Littler is mentioned in [Emily's memoir pp66-67](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#66).
