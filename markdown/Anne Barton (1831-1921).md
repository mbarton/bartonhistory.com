- Born 15 May 1831, daughter of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Married (on 14 September 1865), [Rev. Thomas Henry Fitzpatrick (d.1866)](Rev.%20Thomas%20Henry%20Fitzpatrick%20%28d.1866%29.md).
- Died 1921, aged ~90.
