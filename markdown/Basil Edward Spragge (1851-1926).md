title: "Basil Edward Spragge (1851-1926)"

- Born 9 October 1851, son of [Francis Hoare Spragge (1815-1881)](Francis%20Hoare%20Spragge%20%281815-1881%29.md) and [Sarah Prickett (1812-1899)](Sarah%20Prickett%20%281812-1899%29.md).
- Married, on 15 June 1892 in Kensington, London, [Anne Ingham Brook (b.1871)](Anne%20Ingham%20Brook%20%28b.1871%29.md).
- Died 12 February 1926, in Edinburgh, aged 74.
