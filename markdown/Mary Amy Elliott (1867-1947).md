---
title: "Mary Amy Elliott (1867-1947)"
---

- Born 13 April 1867, eldest daughter of [Sir Charles Alfred Elliott (1835-1911)](Sir%20Charles%20Alfred%20Elliott%20%281835-1911%29.md) and [Louisa Jane Dumbell (1841-1879)](Louisa%20Jane%20Dumbell%20%281841-1879%29.md).
- Known as 'Minnie'.
- Married, in 1891, [Col. Richard Donne](Col.%20Richard%20Donne.md).
- Died 1947, aged ~80.

# Children

With [Col. Richard Donne](Col.%20Richard%20Donne.md):

1. Alice Mary Silvia Donne (b.1892)
2. Raymond Donne (1895-1898)
