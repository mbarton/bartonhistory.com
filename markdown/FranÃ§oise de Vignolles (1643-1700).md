---
title: "Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700)"
---

- Born 2 November 1643, second child and eldest daughter of [Jacques de Vignolles (1609-1686)](Jacques%20de%20Vignolles%20%281609-1686%29.md) and of [Louise de Baschi d’Aubais (1618-1666)](http://familypedia.wikia.com/wiki/Louise_de_Baschi_(1618-1666))
- Married [Jacques Boileau (1626-1697)](Jacques%20Boileau%20%281626-1697%29.md).
- [Descendant of William the Conqueror through her mother](Royal%20ancestry.md#william-the-conqueror-1028-1087-and) [Louise de Baschi d’Aubais (1618-1666)](http://familypedia.wikia.com/wiki/Louise_de_Baschi_(1618-1666))
- Mother of [Charles Boileau (d.1733)](Charles%20Boileau%20%28d.1733%29.md)
- Died on 14 January 1700 in Geneva, aged 56.

Francoise and some of her ancestry is described in **<u>Armorial général de la France, Volume 2 (1742)</u> by Louis-Pierre d' Hozier** (online [here](http://books.google.co.uk/books?id=3Rw-AAAAcAAJ) ):

[[Embedded Google Books viewer]](http://books.google.co.uk/books?id=3Rw-AAAAcAAJ&lpg=PA1048&ots=M-mGXjbY9b&dq=Fran%C3%A7oise%20de%20Vignolles&pg=PA1048&output=embed)

The passage above translates to:

"*The Family History shows that the marriage of Jacques de Vignolles and of Louise de Baschi produced several daughters, of which we know 1. **Françoise de Vignolles** born in 1643 and died in Geneva January 14, 1700 widow of Jacques de Boileau, Seigneur of [Castelnau](Chateau%20Castelnau.md); and 2. Margaret de Vignolles born in 1652, married in 1683 to one Pierre Richard, Seigneur of Vendargues, died in Ireland in 1730.*"
