title: "Lilford Jervoise Causton (b.1873)"

- Born 9 April 1873, son of [Josephina Barton (b.1840)](Josephina%20Barton%20%28b.1840%29.md) and [Rev. Tom Causton (1835-1899)](Rev.%20Tom%20Causton%20%281835-1899%29.md).
- Married 'Winifred'.
- Was 'domestic chaplain' for [Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920)](Rev.%20Handley%20Moule%20%281841-1920%29.md).
