---
title: "Guy Barton (1875-1947)"
---

- Born 5 October 1875, son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Full name 'Guy Douglas Barton'.
- In December 1905 settled into medical practice at [Norwood Lodge](https://maps.google.co.uk/maps?q=Norwood+Lodge,+Weybridge&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Norwood+Lodge,+York+Rd,+Weybridge+KT13+9DS,+United+Kingdom&gl=uk&ei=ZWEGTcLTDYGKhQeu9qnvBw&oi=geocode_result&ved=0CB0Q8gEwAA&z=16), Weybridge.
- Married, in April 1908, [Margaret Horsfall (b.1886)](Margaret%20Horsfall%20%28b.1886%29.md).
- Died 20 June 1947, aged 71.

I'm grateful to Gillian Barton in Australia for providing further information on this family.

# Children

With [Margaret Horsfall (b.1886)](Margaret%20Horsfall%20%28b.1886%29.md):

1. Margaret Muriel(?) Barton (b.1909) - lived in Godalming.
2. Dr. John Douglas Michael Barton (1915-1962) - married Mavis Cooper (later McDonald). They had two children.
