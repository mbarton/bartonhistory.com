---
title: "Katherine Charlotte Grenfell (1842-1906)"
---

- Born 17 December 1842, daughter of [Pascoe St. Leger Grenfell (1798-1874)](Pascoe%20St.%20Leger%20Grenfell%20%281798-1874%29.md) and [Catherine Anne Dupre (b.1804)](Catherine%20Anne%20Dupre%20%28b.1804%29.md).
- Married (on 15 February 1871 in Kilvey, Glamorgan) her first cousin [Henry Edward Thornton (1842-1926)](Henry%20Edward%20Thornton%20%281842-1926%29.md).
- Died 16 January 1906, aged 63.

**Children**

With [Henry Edward Thornton (1842-1926)](Henry%20Edward%20Thornton%20%281842-1926%29.md):

1. Nina Katherine Thornton (1871-1951)
2. Henry Grenfell Thornton (1873-1934)
3. [Susan Thornton (1875-1958)](Susan%20Thornton%20%281875-1958%29.md)
4. Pascoe Spencer Thornton (1877-1917)
5. Claude Cyprian Thornton (1878-1939)
6. Godfrey St. Leger Thornton (1881-1918)
7. Gertrude Fanny Thornton (1882-1967)
8. Rev. John Gordon Thornton (1884-1969)
