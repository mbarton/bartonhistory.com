---
title: "Ellinor Josephina Barton (1857-1940)"
---

- Born 27 July 1857, eldest daughter of [Rev. Joseph Barton (1829-1905)](Rev.%20Joseph%20Barton%20%281829-1905%29.md) and [Ellinor Taylor (1832-1862)](Ellinor%20Taylor%20%281832-1862%29.md).
- Known (at least in [Emily's memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md)) as 'Effie'.
- Married (on 12 January 1893), [Rev. Charles Hamilton](Rev.%20Charles%20Hamilton.md).
- Died 1940, aged ~83.

# Children
