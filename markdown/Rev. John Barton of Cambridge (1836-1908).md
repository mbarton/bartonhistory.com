---
title: "Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x2 grandfather.
- Born 31 December 1836, youngest son of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md) and [Fanny Rickman (1807-1842)](Fanny%20Rickman%20%281807-1842%29.md).
- Contemporary of [W.S.Gilbert (1836-1911)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._S._Gilbert) and [Mark Twain (1835-1920)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Twain).
- Married firstly (in 1859) [Catherine Wigram (1831-1860)](Catherine%20Wigram%20%281831-1860%29.md). She and their [infant daughter](Emily%20Frances%20Barton%20%28b.%26d.1860%29.md) died the year after, as detailed in his [memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md).
- Became missionary in India.
- Married secondly (on 12 October 1863, in [Ludhiana, India](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludhiana)), [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Became vicar of [Holy Trinity Church, Cambridge](Holy%20Trinity%20Church%2C%20Cambridge.md) from 1877-1894 and is still visible in a [stained glass window](Holy%20Trinity%20Church%2C%20Cambridge.md#Stained glass) there.
- Father of [Rev. Cecil Barton (1870-1909)](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md).
- Died 26 November 1908, aged 71.

Stained glass window and plaque at [Holy Trinity Church, Cambridge](Holy%20Trinity%20Church%2C%20Cambridge.md) (to the left of the altar).

John wrote a [memoir](_Memoirs%20of%20John%20Barton.md), and much of his later life is also described in the [memoir of his wife Emily](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md). A [biography of John](_Barton%201910.md) was written by his son [Cecil](Rev.%20Cecil%20Barton%20%281870-1909%29.md) and published posthumously. He has also appeared in the [Dictionary of National Biography](_Dictionary%20of%20National%20Biography.md#).
