---
title: "Bill Barton (1877-1952)"
---

- Born 5 April 1877, youngest surviving son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Full name 'Claude William Boileau Barton' but known as 'Bill' or sometimes 'Will' or 'Willie'.
- Married [Dorothy Cunliffe (b.1884)](Dorothy%20Cunliffe%20%28b.1884%29.md).
- Died 20 October 1952, aged 75.

# Children

With [Dorothy Cunliffe (b.1884)](Dorothy%20Cunliffe%20%28b.1884%29.md):

1. Guy Barton (b.1907)
2. Betty Barton (b.1909)
3. Pippin Barton (b.1916)
4. Joan Barton (b.1918)
