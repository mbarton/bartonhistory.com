---
title: "Catherine Mary Pearson (b.1815)"
---

- Born 12 April 1815, daughter of [Sarah Maria Elliott (1781-1858)](Sarah%20Maria%20Elliott%20%281781-1858%29.md) and [Rev. Hugh Nicholas Pearson (1776-1856)](Rev.%20Hugh%20Nicholas%20Pearson%20%281776-1856%29.md).
- Twin sister of [Henry Hugh Pearson (1815-1873)](Henry%20Hugh%20Pearson%20%281815-1873%29.md).
- Married, in 1844, her first cousin [William Henry Elliott (1811-1870)](William%20Henry%20Elliott%20%281811-1870%29.md).
- Known to [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md) and her family as "Great Aunt Mary Elliott".
- Lived at Holland Park in London.

"*[William Pearson](William%20Pearson%20Elliott%20%281780-1802%29.md), the 2nd Son, had a high post in the Persian Embassy and after distinguishing himself there for a few years, died near Teheran, before he was 30. There is a beautiful chalk drawing of him by Russell in the possession of your **Great Aunt Mary Elliott** of Holland Park. She possesses all the family portraits.*" ([\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p2](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#2))
