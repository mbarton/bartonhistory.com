---
title: "Jacques Boileau (1626-1697)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x8 grandfather.
- Son of [Nicholas Boileau (d.1657)](Nicholas%20Boileau%20%28d.1657%29.md) and [Anne de Calvière de Boucoiran (d.1648)](Anne%20de%20Calvi%C3%A8re%20de%20Boucoiran%20%28d.1648%29.md).
- Married [Françoise de Vignolles (1643-1700)](Fran%C3%A7oise%20de%20Vignolles%20%281643-1700%29.md).
- Father of [Charles Boileau (d.1733)](Charles%20Boileau%20%28d.1733%29.md).

Imprisoned for nearly 10 years in the tower of Pierre-Cise near Lyons, for being a protestant.
