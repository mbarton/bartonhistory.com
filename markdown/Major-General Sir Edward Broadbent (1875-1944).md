---
title: "Major-General Sir Edward Broadbent (1875-1944)"
---

- Born 5 May 1875, second child and eldest son of [Col. John Edward Broadbent (1845-1931)](Col.%20John%20Edward%20Broadbent%20%281845-1931%29.md) and [Dora Nicholson (1844-1897)](Dora%20Nicholson%20%281844-1897%29.md).
- Christened 'Edward Nicholson Broadbent'.
- Became [Lieutenant Governor of Guernsey](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant_Governor_of_Guernsey) (1934-1939), and is in Wikipedia [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Broadbent_(British_Army_officer)) .
- Married Anna Florence ???? (d.1958). No known children.
- Died 18 June 1944, buried at Magdalen Hill Cemetery, Winchester, UK
