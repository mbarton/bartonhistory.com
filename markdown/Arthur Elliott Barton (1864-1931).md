---
title: "Arthur Elliott Barton (1864-1931)"
---

- Born 12 October 1864, eldest son of [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Married firstly [Lilian Wyatt (b.1870)](Lilian%20Wyatt%20%28b.1870%29.md), secondly [Madeleine Parcheminey (b.1885)](Madeleine%20Parcheminey%20%28b.1885%29.md).
- Believed to be the principle author of the [\*Red family tree book](_Red%20family%20tree%20book.md).
- Died 1931, aged ~67.
