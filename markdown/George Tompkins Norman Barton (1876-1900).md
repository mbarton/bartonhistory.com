---
title: "George Tompkins Norman Barton (1876-1900)"
---

- Born 20 April 1876, son of [Rev. Joseph Barton (1829-1905)](Rev.%20Joseph%20Barton%20%281829-1905%29.md) and [Susan Wilhelmina Sullivan (1844-1927)](Susan%20Wilhelmina%20Sullivan%20%281844-1927%29.md).
- Died 16 March 1900, in the [Second Anglo-Boer War](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Anglo-Boer_War) in South Africa, aged 23.
