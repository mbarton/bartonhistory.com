[Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md) ([DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x2 grandmother) was the granddaughter of [Alicia Boileau (1779-1851)](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md). The Boileaus were a [Huguenot](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot) family: minor Protestant nobility who fled France after the [revocation of the Edict of Nantes](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edict_of_Nantes#Revocation) in 1685. Through marriages to various more prominent noble families, the later Boileaus and their descendants can claim descent from royalty. These links were uncovered by the painstaking research of [James Sneyd](http://burningviolin.org/family/index.html), another descendant of the [Boileau family](http://burningviolin.org/family/Boileau/Boileau.htm). Paul Tucker, an Elliott descendant, playfully followed the lines even further, to their (il)logical conclusion!
