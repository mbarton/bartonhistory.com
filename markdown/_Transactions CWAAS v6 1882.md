<u>**Transactions of the Cumberland & Westmorland Antiquarian & Archaeological Society**</u> is a long-standing local history periodical. <u>Volume 6, 1881</u> is available online [here](http://www.archive.org/details/transactionscum09ferggoog). An extract of interest is reproduced below:

**p105**

[[Embedded Internet Archive book viewer]](http://www.archive.org/stream/transactionscum09ferggoog?ui=embed#mode/2up/page/n131)

"*11.-- Barton, as in Machel's notes below, impaling Braithwaite.*"

"*The 11 Barton bears (if this be right) Quarterly 1 Ermine on a fess gules 3 annulets Or—the 2nd Paly of six Arg and vert, the 3 gules between 2 Bends or & ar 3 Lioncels pt arg the 4 gules a chevron Art charged with **3 hurts** inter 3 fleur-de-lis Or.*"

This description is of a Barton coat of arms from [Burneside Hall](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burneside#Burneside_Hall) (see also [here](http://www.britishlistedbuildings.co.uk/en-76431-burneside-hall-strickland-roger/osmap)), which is just north of Kendal:

Why are there [Barton arms](Barton%20arms.md) here? A Thomas Barton of Whenby (possibly a relative of the [Ormside Bartons](Ormside%20Hall.md#Bartons)) married "*Alice, daughter of **Thomas Brathwaite, of Burneside**, and sister of Richard Brathwaite (“Barnabee’s Journall”)*". We don't know any dates for Thomas, but his daughter Elizabeth married Sir Edward Radcliffe (1622-1663). (See [\*Transactions CWAAS v4 1904 pp316-317](_Transactions%20CWAAS%20v4%201904.md))
