---
title: "Antoine Boileau (d.1530)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x12 grandfather.
- Son of [Guillaume Boileau (1420-1494)](Guillaume%20Boileau%20%281420-1494%29.md) and [Etiennette Bourdin](Etiennette%20Bourdin.md).
- Married [Françoise Trousselier](Fran%C3%A7oise%20Trousselier.md).
- Purchased, in 1500, Castelnau-de-la-Garde (now called [Chateau Castelnau](Chateau%20Castelnau.md)-Valence) in southern France near Uzès.
- Father of [Jean Boileau (1500-1562)](Jean%20Boileau%20%281500-1562%29.md).
