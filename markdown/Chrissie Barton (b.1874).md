- Born 31 July 1874, daughter of [Rev. Gerard Barton (1834-1889)](Rev.%20Gerard%20Barton%20%281834-1889%29.md) and Elizabeth Hazard.
- Full name 'Christabel Barton'.
- Married firstly Thomas Ashton Rathbone (1856-1895), son of [William Rathbone VI (1819-1902)](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Rathbone_VI).
- Married secondly [Freddie Payne](Freddie%20Payne.md).
