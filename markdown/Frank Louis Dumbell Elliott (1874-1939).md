- Born 13 September 1874, son of [Sir Charles Alfred Elliott (1835-1911)](Sir%20Charles%20Alfred%20Elliott%20%281835-1911%29.md) and [Louisa Jane Dumbell (1841-1879)](Louisa%20Jane%20Dumbell%20%281841-1879%29.md).
- Married, in 1899, [Mabel Edith Murray (d.1952)](Mabel%20Edith%20Murray%20%28d.1952%29.md).
- Died 1939, aged ~65.
