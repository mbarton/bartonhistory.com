---
title: "William Barton (1737-1818)"
---

- Born 1737 (according to Richard Hargreaves), eldest surviving son of [William Barton (1700-1763)](William%20Barton%20%281700-1763%29.md) and [Frances Setree](Frances%20Setree.md)
- Died on 3 June 1818 in Ivegill, "in his 80th year"?
- Not likely to be the same man as [William Barton of Beacon Hill](William%20Barton%20of%20Beacon%20Hill.md).

# Baptism

The [\*High Head baptismal register](_High%20Head%20baptismal%20register.md) records the following:\
*"Anno 1737"*\
*"August 18 Was William Son of [William Barton](William%20Barton%20%281700-1763%29.md) Baptized."*

Presumably this man. The same register also mentions the 8 February 1735 baptism of "*Jane, Daughter of Wm. Barton*" - his elder sister? (Though Richard Hargreaves has her born in 1732). However there is no sign in the register of younger siblings Isabell (b.1741), Mary (b.1743), [Robert (1747-1795)](Robert%20Barton%20%281747-1795%29.md) or Mary (b.1751). Perhaps they were baptized elsewhere.

# Death Notice

The following death notice is in the <u>**Carlisle Patriot**</u>, Saturday June 6 1818 (held at the British Library):\
*"On Wednesday last, at Ivegill, in his 80th year, Mr. William Barton, much respected."*

If this William were born after 3 June 1737, he would have been 80 years old (technically in his 81st year - but this is an easy sort of mistake to introduce), so it seems likely that this notice does refer to this man.
