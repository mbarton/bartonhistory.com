- The home of [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md).
- Described in the [memoir of his granddaughter Emily Elliott, p7](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#7).
- After renumbering of Portland Place, this became number 61.
