---
title: "William Henry Elliott (1811-1870)"
---

- Born 18 September 1811, second son of [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md) and [Alicia Boileau (1779-1851)](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md).
- Brother of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md).
- Called 'Uncle Willy' by [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Married, in 1844, his first cousin [Catherine Mary Pearson (b.1815)](Catherine%20Mary%20Pearson%20%28b.1815%29.md).
- Died 8 October 1870.

"*I may just mention here that my [Father](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md)'s two brothers followed him to India: '**William Henry**'' who spent 30 years in the Bengal Civil Service — and during his later years was 'Magistrate of the 24 Pergunnas' as it is called - a very large Province in Bengal Proper, & lived at Alipore, a suburb of Calcutta. He married in England his first cousin, [Miss Pearson, daughter of one of the six at Brighton whom I did not enumerate; and most of you know her as 'Great Aunt Mary Elliott'](Catherine%20Mary%20Pearson%20%28b.1815%29.md) who lives now in Holland Park* [Kensington, London]*. She is my Uncle Willie's widow; a very clever, gifted woman, who painted when at Alipore the best collection of Indian flowers that I have ever seen.*" ([\*Memoirs of Emily Elliott p5](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md#5)).
