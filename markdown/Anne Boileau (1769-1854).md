---
title: "Anne Boileau (1769-1854)"
---

- Born 7 November 1769, eldest surviving daughter of [Solomon Boileau (1745-1810)](Solomon%20Boileau%20%281745-1810%29.md) and Dorothea Gradwell.
- Married (on 19 May 1791) [John Reid](John%20Reid.md).
- Died 21 September 1854, aged 84.

# Children

With [John Reid](John%20Reid.md):

1. Henry Solomon Reid (1792-1852)
2. Francis Charlotte Reid (b.1793)
3. Harriet Anne Reid (1794-1853)
4. Sarah Dorothy Reid (b.&d.1795)
5. John Fleming Reid (1796-1797)
6. John Fleming Martin Reid (1797-1859)
7. Elizabeth Reid (b.&d.1798)
8. [Lestock Robert Reid (1799-1878)](Lestock%20Robert%20Reid%20%281799-1878%29.md)
9. Charles Samuel Reid (b.1807)
10. George William Edmondstone Reid (1810-1811)
