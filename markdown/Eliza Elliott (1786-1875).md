---
title: "Eliza Elliott (1786-1875)"
---

- Born 26 October 1786, eldest daughter of [Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832)](Charles%20Elliott%20the%20cabinet-maker%20%281752-1832%29.md) and his second wife [Eling Venn (1758-1843)](Eling%20Venn%20%281758-1843%29.md).
- Married, on 21 July 1812, [Rev. Francis Spragge (d.1838)](Rev.%20Francis%20Spragge%20%28d.1838%29.md).
- Her great grandson [Godfrey John Elliott Spragge (1905-1989)](Godfrey%20John%20Elliott%20Spragge%20%281905-1989%29.md) married [Edith Honor Lister Elliott (b.1903)](Edith%20Honor%20Lister%20Elliott%20%28b.1903%29.md), great great granddaughter of Eliza's half-brother [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md).
- Died 26 February 1875, aged 88.
