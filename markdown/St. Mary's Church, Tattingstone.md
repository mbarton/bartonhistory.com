[Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) was rector here for 38 years from ~1837.

A number of the family are [buried or have memorials here](http://www.gravestonephotos.com/public/cemetery.php?cemetery=84), including [Charles Elliott (1776-1856)](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md) & [Alicia Boileau (1779-1851)](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md), [William Henry Elliott (1811-1870)](William%20Henry%20Elliott%20%281811-1870%29.md), [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) & [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md), [Arthur Wilmot Elliott (1841-1862)](Arthur%20Wilmot%20Elliott%20%281841-1862%29.md), & [Charles Arthur Boileau Elliott (1893-1917)](Charles%20Arthur%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281893-1917%29.md).

*Also of [WILLIAM HENRY ELLIOTT](William%20Henry%20Elliott%20%281811-1870%29.md), ESQUIRE of H.M. Bengal Civil Service, Second Son of the said [CHARLES ELLIOTT](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md) ESQUIRE who died on the 8th October 1870, Aged 59 Years.*\

![St Marys Tattingstone monument to Alicia Boileau.jpg](../files/St%20Marys%20Tattingstone%20monument%20to%20Alicia%20Boileau.jpg "St Marys Tattingstone monument to Alicia Boileau.jpg")\
Image courtesy of Charles Sale, [[@<http://www.gravestonephotos.com/public/gravedetails.php?available=yes&fullname=Charles> Elliott&grave=13639|www.gravestonephotos.com]].

*NEAR THIS TABLET REPOSE THE MORTAL REMAINS OF [ALICIA](Alicia%20Boileau%20%281779-1851%29.md). FOR NEARLY FIFTY YEARS THE LOVING AND TENDERLY BELOVED WIFE OF [CHARLES ELLIOTT](Charles%20Elliott%20%281776-1856%29.md) ESQUIRE, OF THE EAST INDIA COMPANY'S BENGAL CIVIL SERVICE.*\
*DESCENDED FROM THE ELDEST BRANCH OF THE [BOILEAUS](_Chronicles%20of%20Boileau.md) DE CASTELNAU, OF THE PROVINCE OF LANGUEDOC, WHO, AT THE REVOCATION OF THE EDICT OF NANTES, CHOOSING RATHER TO ABANDON THEIR COUNTRY THAN THE TRUTH OF GOD, TOOK REFUGE IN IRELAND FROM ROMISH PERSECUTION.*\
*SHE UNITED THE VIVACITY AND BRILLIANCY OF THE RACE FROM WHICH SHE SPRANG WITH THE TALENT AND WARMTH OF HEART OF THAT WITH WHICH SHE BLENDED IN BIRTH; WHILE THE QUALITIES, WHICH ESPECIALLY CAPTIVATED AND ENDEARED HER TO ALL WERE RENUNCIATION OF SELF AND CONSIDERATION OF OTHERS.*\
*AFTER A LONG LIFE OF ALMOST UNINTERRUPTED HEALTH AND ENJOYMENT, ERE AGE WITH ITS WITHERING TOUCH HAD IMPAIRED A SENSE OR DEADENED A FEELING, SHE WAS SUDDENLY STRICKEN BY SEVERE DISEASE; UNDER WHICH AN UNRUFFLED CALMNESS IN THE ANTICIPATION OF DEATH MARKED THE STRENGTH OF HER FAITH IN HER REDEEMER'S SACRIFICE, WHILE HER CONFIDENCE IN HIS LOVE CONVERTED INCREASING SUFFERINGS INTO OCCASIONS OF INCREASING PRAISE.*\
*BLAMELESS AS SHE APPEARED TO OTHERS IN ALL THE RELATIONS OF LIFE, SHE YET SAW HERSELF IN THE LIGHT OF GOD'S WORD, A SINNER JUSTLY CONDEMNED. AS SUCH, SHE CAST HERSELF AT THE FOOT OF THE CROSS OF CHRIST;* *AND, PLEADING HIS ONLY MERITS* *WITH A HOPE FULL OF IMMORTALITY* *SHE FELL ASLEEP IN HIM* *ON THE 5TH DAY OF JULY 1851,* *IN THE 72ND YEAR OF HER AGE.*\
[N.B. The vault memorial (above) says she was 71. If her birth year was 1779 then she was 71 years and 10 months old. Of course if you are 71 then you are indeed technically in your 72nd year.]

*FOR MANY YEARS A MAGISTRATE AND DEPUTY LIEUTENANT OF THE COUNTY OF MIDDLESEX, AND FORMERLY OF THE BENGAL CIVIL SERVICE; WHO DIED ON THE 4TH DAY OF MAY, 1856, IN THE 80TH YEAR OF HIS AGE.*\
