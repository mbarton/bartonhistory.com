---
title: "Simeon Boileau (1717-1767)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David%20Barton)'s great x6 grandfather.
- Born 10 October 1717, middle child of [Charles Boileau (d.1733)](Charles%20Boileau%20%28d.1733%29.md).
- Lived in Dublin.
- Married, on 6 August 1741, [Magdelana Desbrisay (1720-1786)](Magdelana%20Desbrisay%20%281720-1786%29.md).
- Father of [Solomon Boileau (1745-1810)](Solomon%20Boileau%20%281745-1810%29.md).
- Died 1 July 1767, aged 49 years and 9 months. Buried at the [Huguenot Cemetery](Huguenot%20Cemetery%2C%20Dublin.md) on Merrion Row in Dublin.

# Grave

Simeon's grave at the [Huguenot Cemetery, Dublin](Huguenot%20Cemetery%2C%20Dublin.md) carries the following inscription:

"*HERE LIETH THE REMAINS OF Mr SIMEON BOILEAU WHO DIED 1st JULY 1767 AGED 50 YEARS.*\
*ALSO THE REMAINS OF THE LATE Mr JOHN BOILEAU HIS BROTHER AND THOSE OF THE LATE THEOPHILUS DESBRISAY ESQ.*\
*THIS STONE WAS ERECTED TO THE MEMORY OF THE ABOVE SIMEON, BY HIS SON THOMAS BOILEAU*"

This is reproduced in [Parkinson 1988](Huguenot%20Cemetery%2C%20Dublin.md#boileau-burial-plot) along with a note saying that "*Mem. of the Dead Vol III p538* ["Journal for the Preservation of the Memorials of the Dead"?] *says that the burials register shows that John Boileau died 1 Feb 1772 aged 50 years and Capt Theophile Desbrisay died 5 July 1772 aged 79 years."*

This conflicts with the currently known [list of Simeon's siblings](Charles%20Boileau%20%28d.1733%29.md#children), which contains only one 'Jean', who supposedly died 1722. Possibly this error was introduced by a misreading of 1772 as 1722.
