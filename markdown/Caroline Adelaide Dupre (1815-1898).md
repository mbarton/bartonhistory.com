---
title: "Caroline Adelaide Dupre (1815-1898)"
---

- Born 3 June 1815, daughter of [James Dupre (1778-1870)](James%20Dupre%20%281778-1870%29.md) and [Madeline Maxwell (1780-1818)](Madeline%20Maxwell%20%281780-1818%29.md).
- Married [Rev. Spencer Ruthven Thornton (1813-1850)](Rev.%20Spencer%20Ruthven%20Thornton%20%281813-1850%29.md).
- Died 17 February 1898, aged 82.
