---
title: "Charles Pearson Elliott (1833-1876)"
---

- Born 10 December 1833, second child and eldest son of [Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875)](Rev.%20Charles%20Boileau%20Elliott%20%281803-1875%29.md) and [Emily Dougan (1806-1877)](Emily%20Dougan%20%281806-1877%29.md).
- Elder brother of [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md), mentioned in her [memoirs](_Memoirs%20of%20Emily%20Elliott.md) usually as 'Charlie' or 'Uncle Charles'.
- Married (on 21 May 1862) [Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843)](Hannah%20Frances%20Lister%20%28b.1843%29.md) (who was usually called Fannie).
- Died 1 July 1876, aged 42.
