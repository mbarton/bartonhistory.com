---
title: "Arthur Edward Elliott (b.1867)"
---

- Born 4 August 1867, son of [Charles Pearson Elliott (1833-1876)](Charles%20Pearson%20Elliott%20%281833-1876%29.md) and [Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843)](Hannah%20Frances%20Lister%20%28b.1843%29.md).
- Married (on 3 July 1895) Janet Harriet Smith (see picture below).
- Organist for [Rev. Handley Moule (1841-1920)](Rev.%20Handley%20Moule%20%281841-1920%29.md).
