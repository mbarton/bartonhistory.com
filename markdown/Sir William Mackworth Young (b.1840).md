---
title: "Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840)"
---

- Born 15 August 1840, son of Sir George Young.
- Friends with [Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908)](Rev.%20John%20Barton%20of%20Cambridge%20%281836-1908%29.md) and [Emily Elliott (1839-1924)](Emily%20Elliott%20%281839-1924%29.md).
- Lived in India.
- Married, firstly, Emily's sister [Isabel Maria Elliott (1842-1870)](Isabel%20Maria%20Elliott%20%281842-1870%29.md).
- Married, secondly, [Frances May Egerton](Frances%20May%20Egerton.md).
- Died about 1924?
