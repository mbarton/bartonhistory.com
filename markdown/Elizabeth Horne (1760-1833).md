---
title: "Elizabeth Horne (1760-1833)"
---

- [DBHB](Living%20connections.md#David Barton)'s great x4 grandmother.
- Daughter of [Thomas Horne (1725-1802)](Thomas%20Horne%20%281725-1802%29.md) and [Mary Hill (1734-1798)](Mary%20Hill%20%281734-1798%29.md).
- Married (on 13 December 1787), [John Barton the Elder (1754-1789)](John%20Barton%20the%20Elder%20%281754-1789%29.md), being his second wife.
- Mother of [John Barton Senior (1789-1852)](John%20Barton%20Senior%20%281789-1852%29.md), and stepmother to [Bernard Barton the Quaker poet (1784-1849)](Bernard%20Barton%20the%20Quaker%20poet%20%281784-1849%29.md) and [Maria Hack (1777-1844)](Maria%20Hack%20%281777-1844%29.md).
- Died 20 August 1833, aged ~73.
