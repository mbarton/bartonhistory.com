The [Dublin Boileaus](Simeon%20Boileau%20%281717-1767%29.md) are buried in the [Huguenot](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot) Cemetery on Merrion Row in [Dublin](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin) (also known as the "French cemetery"). A list of the surnames of all individuals buried there can be found through <http://www.huguenotsinireland.com/huguenotburial.html>. The cemetery has its own Wikipedia entry [here](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huguenot_cemetery,_Dublin).

# Gravestone of [Simeon Boileau](Simeon%20Boileau%20%281717-1767%29.md)

Simeon's grave is marked on the map above with a red **X**. Parkinson 1988 (see above) contains a copy, credited to Dr. Michael J. S. Egan, of the inscription:

"*HERE LIETH THE REMAINS OF Mr **SIMEON BOILEAU** WHO DIED 1st JULY 1767 AGED 50 YEARS.*\
*ALSO THE REMAINS OF THE LATE Mr JOHN BOILEAU HIS BROTHER AND THOSE OF THE LATE THEOPHILUS DESBRISAY ESQ.*\
*THIS STONE WAS ERECTED TO THE MEMORY OF THE ABOVE SIMEON, BY HIS SON THOMAS BOILEAU*"

The booklet also notes that "*Mem. of the Dead Vol III p538* ["Journal for the Preservation of the Memorials of the Dead"?] *says that the burials register shows that John Boileau died 1 Feb 1772 aged 50 years and Capt Theophile Desbrisay died 5 July 1772 aged 79 years.*"

This conflicts with the currently known [list of Simeon's siblings](Charles%20Boileau%20%28d.1733%29.md#children), which contains only one 'Jean', who supposedly died 1722. Possibly this error was introduced by a misreading of 1772 as 1722.
