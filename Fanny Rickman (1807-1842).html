• DBHB's great x3 grandmother.
  • Born ~1807, daughter of Quakers Joseph Rickman (d.1808) and Sarah Rickman, who were second cousins.
  • Full name 'Frances Rickman' but always known as 'Fanny'.
  • Married (at Smeeth Hill House, near Ashford in Kent, on 23 July 1828) John Barton Senior (1789-1852).
  • Mother of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908).
  • Died 14 November 1842, aged ~35.
  • Her sister Josephina Rickman (1808-1892) became a foster mother to her children, and in fact became sole guardian after their father also died ten years later.
