• Claimed as an ancestor of Jean Boileau (d.1396) - supposedly his great great grandfather - making him DBHB's great x20 grandfather.
  • Born either in 1200 or 1210.
  • The earliest recorded 'Boileau', which may have led later Boileaus to invent a connection to him, though no supporting evidence now exists (according to the *Chronicles of Boileau).
  • Provost of Paris from 1261, appointed by King Louis IX of France (Saint Louis). Etienne's wikipedia entry is here.
  • Died ~1269-1271.
