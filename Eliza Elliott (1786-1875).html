• Born 26 October 1786, eldest daughter of Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832) and his second wife Eling Venn (1758-1843).
  • Married, on 21 July 1812, Rev. Francis Spragge (d.1838).
  • Her great grandson Godfrey John Elliott Spragge (1905-1989) married Edith Honor Lister Elliott (b.1903), great great granddaughter of Eliza's half-brother Charles Elliott (1776-1856).
  • Died 26 February 1875, aged 88.
