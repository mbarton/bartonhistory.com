• Born 29 September 1860, daughter of Lestock Robert Reid (1799-1878) and Jane Boileau.
  • Married a vicar in England.
  • Died 10 June 1900, aged 39.

"It was a sad break in our party when Lilford was sent to return to England on account of the death of his Vicar's wife, a cousin of mine, née Ethel Reid." *Memoirs of Emily Elliott p120
