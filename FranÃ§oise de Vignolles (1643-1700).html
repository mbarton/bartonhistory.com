• Born 2 November 1643, second child and eldest daughter of Jacques de Vignolles (1609-1686) and of Louise de Baschi d’Aubais (1618-1666)
  • Married Jacques Boileau (1626-1697).
  • Descendant of William the Conqueror through her mother Louise de Baschi d’Aubais (1618-1666)
  • Mother of Charles Boileau (d.1733)
  • Died on 14 January 1700 in Geneva, aged 56.


Francoise and some of her ancestry is described in Armorial général de la France, Volume 2 (1742) by Louis-Pierre d' Hozier (online here ):



The passage above translates to:

"The Family History shows that the marriage of Jacques de Vignolles and of Louise de Baschi produced several daughters, of which we know 1. Françoise de Vignolles born in 1643 and died in Geneva January 14, 1700 widow of Jacques de Boileau, Seigneur of Castelnau; and 2. Margaret de Vignolles born in 1652, married in 1683 to one Pierre Richard, Seigneur of Vendargues, died in Ireland in 1730."
