• Born 23 December 1841, son of Rev. Henry Moule (1801-1880).
  • Full name 'Handley Carr Glyn Moule'.
  • A good friend and colleague of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908).
  • Married, on 16 August 1881, John's sister-in-law Mary Elliott (b.1844).
  • Became Bishop of Durham (1901-1920), from whence he followed tradition by signing his name 'H. Dunelm' (the Latin for Durham).
  • His wikipedia entry is here.
  • Wrote the preface in 'John Barton: a Memoir (1910)' by John's son Cecil.
  • Died 8 May 1920, aged 78.
