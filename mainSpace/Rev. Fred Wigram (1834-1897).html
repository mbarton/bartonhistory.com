• Born 1 June 1834, son of Edward Wigram (b.1802) and Catherine Smith.
  • Good friend of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) who married Fred's sister. Mentioned repeatedly in John's memoirs.
  • Married (on 31 May 1859, at Osmaston near Ashbourne, Derbyshire), Frances Wright (1840-1914).
  • Died 10 March 1897, aged 62.

More info on Fred and his family can be found at http://www.highfieldhistory.co.uk/#/rev-frederic-edward-wigram/4537869766 and also at http://www.thepeerage.com/p46075.htm#i460744.
