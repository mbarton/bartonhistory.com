• Born 9 March 1842, daughter of Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875) and Emily Dougan (1806-1877).
  • Sister of Emily Elliott (1839-1924), who refers to her as 'Aunt Isa' in her memoirs.
  • Married, on 17 August 1869, Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840).
  • Mother of Isabel Mary Young (b.1870).
  • Died young, 28 May 1870, aged 28. Her husband remarried in 1881.
