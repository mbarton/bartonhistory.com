• Born 27 September 1781, daughter of Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832) and Sarah Ann Sherman (d.1784).
  • Married (on 13 September 1803), Rev. Hugh Nicholas Pearson (1776-1856).
  • Her daughter Catherine married Sarah's nephew William Henry Elliott (1811-1870).
  • Died in 1858, aged ~77.
