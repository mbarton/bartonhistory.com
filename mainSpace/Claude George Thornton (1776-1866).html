• Born 20 January 1776, son of Godfrey Thornton (1737-1805) and Jane Codin.
  • Married (on 18 April 1806 at St Martin-in-the-Fields, Westminster) Frances Anne Smith (1785-1862).
  • Died 4 August 1866, aged 90.

Children


With Frances Anne Smith (1785-1862):
  1. Frances Thornton (b.&d.1806).
  2. George Smith Thornton (1808-1867).
  3. Sophia Thornton (1811-1827).
  4. Rev. Spencer Ruthven Thornton (1813-1850).
  5. Charles Thornton (1827-1851).
