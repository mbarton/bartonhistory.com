• Born 10 December 1833, second child and eldest son of Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875) and Emily Dougan (1806-1877).
  • Elder brother of Emily Elliott (1839-1924), mentioned in her memoirs usually as 'Charlie' or 'Uncle Charles'.
  • Married (on 21 May 1862) Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843) (who was usually called Fannie).
  • Died 1 July 1876, aged 42.




Children


With Hannah Frances Lister (b.1843):
  1. Frances Elizabeth Boileau Elliott (b.&d.1863).
  2. Charles Lister Boileau Elliott (1864-1940).
  3. Alicia Mary Elliott (b.&d.1866).
  4. Arthur Edward Elliott (b.1867).
  5. Edith Mary Elliott (b.1869).
  6. William Robert Elliott (b.1871).
