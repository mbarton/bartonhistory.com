• DBHB's great x3 grandfather.
  • Born 11 March 1796, son of John Broadbent (1758-1837) and Mary Dyson (d.1805).
  • Contemporary of Emperor Wilhelm I of Germany (1797-1888) and Mary Shelley (1797-1851).
  • Married (on 17 May 1832, in Longwood, Huddersfield), Esther Butterworth (1810-1879).
  • Died 1880, aged ~84.
