• Born 12 October 1864, eldest son of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) and Emily Elliott (1839-1924).
  • Married firstly Lilian Wyatt (b.1870), secondly Madeleine Parcheminey (b.1885).
  • Believed to be the principle author of the *Red family tree book.
  • Died 1931, aged ~67.
