• Born 1840, daughter of Francis Wright (1806-1873) and Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888).
  • Known to Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) as 'Fanny', and is mentioned in his memoirs.
  • Married (on 31 May 1859, at Osmaston near Ashbourne, Derbyshire), Rev. Fred Wigram (1834-1897). It was a double wedding with her sister Elizabeth Wright (d.1909), and Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) attended and described it in his memoirs.
  • Died 16 February 1914, aged 74.
