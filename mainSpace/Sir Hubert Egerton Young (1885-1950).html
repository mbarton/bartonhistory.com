• Born 1885, second son of Sir William Mackworth Young (b.1840) and Frances May Egerton.
  • Married in 1924 Margaret Rose Mary Reynolds, and had three sons with her.
  • Governor of Nyasaland (Malawi) 1932-1934.
  • Governor of Northern Rhodesia 1935-1938.
  • Governor of Trinidad and Tobago 1938-1942.
  • Author of "The Independent Arab".

  • Died 20 April 1950 in Portugal, aged ~65.
