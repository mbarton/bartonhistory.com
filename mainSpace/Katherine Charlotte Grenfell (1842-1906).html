• Born 17 December 1842, daughter of Pascoe St. Leger Grenfell (1798-1874) and Catherine Anne Dupre (b.1804).
  • Married (on 15 February 1871 in Kilvey, Glamorgan) her first cousin Henry Edward Thornton (1842-1926).
  • Died 16 January 1906, aged 63.
