• DBHB's great x5 grandmother.
  • Born 1745 in Dublin, Ireland, daughter of Francis Gladwell.
  • Married, on 3 May 1766, Solomon Boileau (1745-1810).
  • Died 4 May 1789 in Dublin, Ireland, aged 44.


Gradwell or Gladwell?


Solomon and Dorothy are listed in ThePeerage.com . This gives her name as "Dorothea Gradwell". James Sneyd gives her name as "Dorothy Gladwell". The family history website at www.holmesacourt.org (which also gives her dates as shown here) gives her name as "Dorothea Gladwell", and this is presumed the correct spelling (for now!)
