• Born 7 February 1758, daughter of Henry Venn (1725-1797).
  • Married, on 2 December 1785, widower Charles Elliott the cabinet-maker (1752-1832).
  • Mother of Charlotte Elliott (1789-1871) and Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875).
  • Great aunt of John Venn (1834-1923) the mathematician and inventor of Venn diagrams.
  • Died 17 April 1843, aged 85.
