• Born 5 April 1877, youngest surviving son of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) and Emily Elliott (1839-1924).
  • Full name 'Claude William Boileau Barton' but known as 'Bill' or sometimes 'Will' or 'Willie'.
  • Married Dorothy Cunliffe (b.1884).
  • Died 20 October 1952, aged 75.


Children


With Dorothy Cunliffe (b.1884):
  1. Guy Barton (b.1907)
  2. Betty Barton (b.1909)
  3. Pippin Barton (b.1916)
  4. Joan Barton (b.1918)
