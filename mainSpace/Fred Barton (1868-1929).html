• Born 28 September 1868, son of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) and Emily Elliott (1839-1924).
  • Full name 'Percy Frederick Barton', but always known as 'Fred'.
  • Married (on 23 December 1896 in Norbiton Church near Kingston), Anne Stote Fox (1867-1957).
  • Died May 1929, aged 60.

Named after Rev. Frederick Gell, Bishop of Madras (1820-1902) (*Barton 1910)

Children


With Anne Stote Fox (1867-1957):
  1. Emily Marjory Barton (b.1897)
  2. Humphrey Douglas Elliott Barton (b.1900)
  3. David Gerard Frederick Barton (b.1901)
  4. George Donald Barton (b.1904)
  5. Violet Jean Barton (b.1908)
