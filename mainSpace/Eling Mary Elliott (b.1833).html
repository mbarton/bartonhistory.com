• Born 21 April 1833, daughter of Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875) and Mary King.
  • Named after her grandmother Eling Venn (1758-1843).
  • Married, on 1 July 1856, Rev. Clement Frances Cobb (1827-1896)

She was a first (half-)cousin of Rev. Charles Boileau Elliott (1803-1875) and thus the second (half-)cousin once removed of his daughter Emily Elliott (1839-1924). Her son Frank married Emily Gertrude Causton (b.1869), who was Emily's husband's niece.


Children


With Rev. Clement Frances Cobb (1827-1896):
  1. Clement Francis Edward Cobb (b.1857)
  2. Edward Elliott Cobb (b.1859)
  3. John Bartlett Cobb (1863-1895)
  4. Henry Venn Cobb (b.1865)
  5. Rev. Frank William Cobb (b.1872) [who married Emily Gertrude Causton (b.1869) daughter of Josephina Barton (b.1840)]
