• Son of Francis Wright (1806-1873) and Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888).
  • Good friend of Rev. John Barton of Cambridge (1836-1908) and mentioned repeatedly in his memoirs as 'Frank'.
  • Married, on 20 June 1862, his first cousin Adeline Frances Henrietta FitzHerbert.
  • Died 1911.

I'm indebted to Charles Wright, a modern relative, for the details of his marriage and wife, and of a guest list - written down by Frank - of the double wedding of his sisters Bessie and Fanny at Osmaston Manor in 1859.
