• Born 5 September 1828, eldest son of Rev. Edward Bishop Elliott (1793-1875) and his first wife Mary King.
  • Married, on 5 July 1853, Mary Elizabeth Richey.
  • Rector of St. Mary's in Broadwater, Sussex, from 1853-1905.
  • Died 31 October 1920, aged 92. Buried at Broadwater and Worthing Cemetery.

Portrait


The image of the following portrait, from St. Mary's Church in Broadwater, was kindly provided by Mrs Christina Taylor with help and permission from Rev. Irwin-Clark. Though there was no caption, they believed it to be Edward King Elliott based on its position in a chronological series of portraits of previous Rectors at the church, (and there was a familial resemblance to his father). Mrs Taylor was then able to confirm his identity because there is another photograph of Edward, clearly from the same sitting, held by Worthing Reference Library (Richmond Road, Worthing, BN11 1HD). This other photograph was taken by Elliott and Fry Studio (no relation?) of 55 Baker Street, London. On the reverse was written: "Rev. E. K. Elliott" (with "or Elliot?" added in pencil in a different hand) "Rector of Broadwater 1853-1905 - died 1920".




Gravestone


This image of his shared gravestone was kindly provided by Mrs Christina Taylor of 'The Friends of Broadwater & Worthing Cemetery' (www.fbwc.co.uk/):



The text reads:

"Sacred to the dearly loved memory of Julia Louisa Richey Elliott. Second daughter of the Rev. E. K. & Mrs. Elliott who entered into the joy of her Lord at Broadwater Rectory February 27th 1905.

Sacred also to the universally beloved memory of Mary. Wife of the Rev. E. K. Elliott who departed this life at the Rectory to be with Christ: Novr. 2nd 1909.

"Her children arise up and call her blessed"

Also to the memory of the Rev. E. K. Elliott, for 52 years Rector of Broadwater, who passed away on Oct. 31st 1920. Aged 92 years."
