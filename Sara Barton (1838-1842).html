• Born 1838, daughter of John Barton Senior (1789-1852) and Fanny Rickman (1807-1842).
  • Some dispute about her name. Her father called her 'Sara' in a letter he wrote, but her brother John spelled it 'Sarah' in his memoirs. It is assumed the former is correct.
  • Died 9 November 1842, aged ~4, of scarlet fever which also killed her mother five days later.
