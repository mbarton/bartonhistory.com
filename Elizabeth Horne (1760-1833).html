• DBHB's great x4 grandmother.
  • Daughter of Thomas Horne (1725-1802) and Mary Hill (1734-1798).
  • Married (on 13 December 1787), John Barton the Elder (1754-1789), being his second wife.
  • Mother of John Barton Senior (1789-1852), and stepmother to Bernard Barton the Quaker poet (1784-1849) and Maria Hack (1777-1844).
  • Died 20 August 1833, aged ~73.
