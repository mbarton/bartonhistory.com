• Born 21 December 1806, son of John Wright (1758-1840) and Elizabeth Beresford (1762-1833).
  • Married (on 12 August 1830) Selina FitzHerbert (1806-1888).
  • Lived at Osmaston Manor, Derbyshire.
  • Died 21 February 1873, aged 66.



Mr Francis Wright of Osmaston Manor. He was High Sheriff of Nottinghamshire in 1842. Image from http://www.thepeerage.com/p30478.htm#i304779, which provides a lot more information about his family.
